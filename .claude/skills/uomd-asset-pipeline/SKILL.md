# uomd-asset-pipeline

Follow [pipeline standards](../../standards/asset-pipeline.md) and [audit/promotion workflow](../../workflows/asset-audit.md). Inspect existing tools/IDs/manifests before inventing conventions. Preserve creative staging, source masters, read-only Production/LEGACY and locked references. Validate technical/identity/license/approval status, record provenance and base/patch class, then promote only within authorized local scope. Do not push/publish, change modality models or call paid external services because of a historical template.

For the reviewed shared-source additions, read [art-production guidance](references/shared-art-production.md).
