## Open mysteries (do not resolve without a decision)

- Where the Clockless Court is, and what Mahmut's "one hour" is.
- Who silenced the Silent Bell, and why (the bell itself is known: the capital's).
- Who killed King Audren. In-world it is unknowable (inside the Unwritten Hour, C26); the design answer must be locked before the Part II outline.

## Decisions C15–C21 (world foundation W1, approved by the owner 2026-09-29)

- **C15 The Ledger.** The seven bells are one ledger; unwitnessed dead become Hollows; a bell is a place, not bronze (recast bells and shards keep counting); distance slows payment and never stops it. (`world_rules.md` 6, 7, 9)
- **C16 The bell line.** Only the Veyr bell line, mother to daughter, can be sealed as Last Bell. Cael is Veyr by birth, not of the bell line. F-012 reworded.
- **C17 Terms.** Last Bell, bearer, Last Bell seal, the two meanings of "heir", the Offering, the Sevenfold Crown, the First Eclipse; "the Unwritten Hour" means only Cael's stolen hour. (`glossary.md`)
- **C18 The Silent Bell and the seven.** The capital bell fell silent in the throne wars at least 15 years before Part I; seven bell-line daughters were sealed to stand in for it and spent at the Altar within about ten years.
- **C19 Crownfall's seal.** Fadyme was sealed at the bell altar beneath the Silent Bell; the second order sent her to the First Eclipse Altar (Yseldra carried, Oren signed, Mahmut defied).
- **C20 Cael's machinery.** The Unwritten Hour / the Rewrite / the Hinge / the Schedule. Public promise: the minute before Crownfall. Hidden aim (SECRET until Part II): keep counting back to the hour he lost Liora and Aren.
- **C21 Calling bells.** A calling bell stays tuned to its bearer and sounds when her debt moves; Yseldra's tied bell is how she tracks Fadyme.

## Decisions C22–C28 (world foundation W2, approved by the owner 2026-09-29)

- **C22 The throne wars.** A ducal succession war over King Audren of House Carrow, who had no legitimate children; the Silent Bell fell silent during it.
- **C23 Fadyme's royal years.** Secretly recognized at 7 and raised in a guarded royal hunting lodge; publicly declared at 13 and brought to court, which opened the last and bloodiest phase of the wars; Mahmut assigned as her shield. Resolves CC-04.
- **C24 The orders.** The king's last page is Cael's unanswered letter; the king refused. After his death Cael, as chancellor, issued both Crownfall orders under the Inquisition seal.
- **C25 The Council of Dukes.** Formed after Crownfall as a truce of seven ducal houses; the throne stays empty because any living heir restarts the war. Duke names: later.
- **C26 The stolen hour hides the king's death and Cael's name.** World rule 11. Resolves LK-01; the killer's identity is locked before the Part II outline.
- **C27 Oren.** Paid by the Council to finish the Crownfall order; obeys because it is unfinished. Resolves LK-12.
- **C28 The portrait night.** The mother's portrait in the king's study fell on Crownfall night (`ch1_camp`).
