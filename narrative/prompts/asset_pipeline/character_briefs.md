# Character Identity Blocks (for production prompts)

Extracted from approved sheets. A block may only be used once its sheet is approved.

Each block has: IDENTITY (what the sheet shows), ADDITIONS (canon details the sheet lacks; optional), PALETTE, PLAY ACCENT, LOCKS (what must never drift), KEY COLOUR. Production prompts quote IDENTITY + ADDITIONS verbatim and end with a "Locks:" sentence.

## fadyme — APPROVED (art/sheets/char-fadyme-uomd.png)
IDENTITY: adult woman (22), slender, composed, gentle fatigue in the eyes; long pale ash-blonde hair falling from under a deep plum hood; violet eyes; small broken silver crown fragment pinned at the front edge of the hood above her brow (where the sheet shows it; lore calls this "the hood clasp"); deep plum layered hooded mantle with silver-lilac floral embroidery at the hems, the same embroidery on the long skirt of a high-collared black dress; white scarf worn high at the throat; thin dark gloves; a silver bell hanging on a chain (its clapper is a small silver thorn).
PALETTE: plum, aubergine, black, silver, white. PLAY ACCENT: pale lilac-violet (her veil magic).
ADDITIONS (canon, not on the sheet): a tiny silver bell-shaped hairpin in her hair at the side of the face, half hidden by the hood (House Veyr pin, A-004; "keep her hood up", ch5_road). Always small; shown clearly only when the hood slips.
LOCKS: pale ash-blonde hair (never dark); hood up in default views; bell with a thorn clapper that never swings; no curse marks unless a story state is requested; the bone-white lock (from 5-4) stays hidden under the hood, so default art does not show it.
EXPRESSIONS: composed (default, her mask), quiet_pain, honest_fear, warm (`pilot_fadyme.md`).
KEY COLOUR: #00FF00.

IDENTITY: adult man (28), tall, broad-shouldered weathered knight; messy dark brown hair, almost black; light stubble; tired watchful brown eyes; dark worn half-plate over a dark gambeson, repaired in mismatched styles; tattered oxblood cloak wrapped high like a cowl; no helmet; long arming sword with brown grip; dark iron heater shield with a raised four-point star and a riveted patch plate.
ADDITIONS (canon, unclear on the sheet): small Crown Guard star on the breastplate with the empty hammered socket above it (A-003); the sword's hilt crest socket plugged with plain iron (weapon lore); a small gray wax seal on the shield rim (Yseldra, opening; 3-5, 7-1).

LOCKS: empty socket above the star, never a crown on his armor; shield visibly repaired; hair always dark; no helmet; cloak oxblood and torn, never fur.
EXPRESSIONS: watchful (default), dry_humor, protective_resolve, gentle_concern, grief (`mahmut.md`).
