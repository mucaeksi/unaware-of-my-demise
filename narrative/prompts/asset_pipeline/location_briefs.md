# Location Briefs (narrative team)

Each brief: palette, key shapes, focal landmark, and what ORDER_LEVEL changes. Crimson only as omen marks.

## ch1 — forest_whispering_ash (Whispering Ash Forest)
Damp overgrown forest: layered mossy and dark pine greens under a pale ash-grey canopy of ash trees whose pale leaves look like drifting ash; rain and low mist; a narrow muddy road; old trunks with names carved into the bark; a small old stone roadside wayshrine the Church has left behind but one old keeper still tends (ch1_first_oath, cs_brin_alder_2: weathered stone, swept doorstep, a register board inside, one shuttered lantern) with a small empty bell niche under its eave; the distant spire of the Saint Luthos bell tower above the trees; one warm camp-light inside the crop-safe area. The alder with Bram Alder's carved name stands behind the shrine.
- ordered: road clear, carved names readable, shrine intact, light mist.
- uneasy: a few fallen trunks across the road, carved names partly worn away, thicker mist, a few pale ash flakes drifting upward instead of down.
- chaotic: road blocked by fallen trunks, shrine roof collapsed, heavy mist, bell niche cracked, thin crimson omen marks on a few trunks.

## ch2 — cathedral_saint_luthos
Broken rose window, flooded nave reflecting grey light, rows of candles on stone, a bell tower with no rope. Palette: cold stone grey, dawn gold candlelight, deep blue shadow.

## ch3 — village_oakhaven
Orchard walls, bare apple trees with fruit still on the branches, a windmill, a village well, white linen drying in the square, blank wooden name boards. Palette: ochre, faded green, white linen, dusk rose.

## ch4 — undercity_subterranean
Drainage canals, mushroom lamps, still cistern water, a hidden clinic ward behind a storage door. Palette: teal-black water, mushroom amber, damp stone.

## ch5 — bridge_forsaken
A great stone span over a fog-filled ravine, torn banners, abandoned siege winches and chains, a signal tower. Palette: fog white, slate, iron.

## ch6 — estate_ashen_lord
Soot-dark manor, portrait gallery with nameplates cut away, a glass conservatory with cracked panes, a half-collapsed archive. Palette: soot black, tarnished gold, cold glass teal.

## ch7 — altar_first_eclipse
A crown-shaped crater, seven broken bell chains, a circular black-stone altar, an unmoving eclipse overhead (the one place where a crimson ring is allowed as the focal omen). Palette: black stone, ash grey, the crimson eclipse ring.

## Side roads (companion stories and events; written 2026-09-30)

Single state (ordered) unless a scene asks otherwise. Same painting language as the chapter hubs; crimson only as an omen mark.

### lowland_refuge (cs_brin_alder_3 "Two Roofs")
A temporary refuge in the western lowlands for woodcutters and villagers from the forest road: two long timber-and-canvas shelters side by side on a muddy rise, one roof visibly sagging, spare beams stacked by the doorways, cook-fires under lean-tos, laundry lines, rain. Palette: wet timber brown, canvas off-white, rain grey, the warm orange of cook-fires. Focal: the sagging roof beam. Mood: people making do, not despair.

### lowland_refuge_road (cs_marek_vane_2, cs_nara_vell_3)
The long lowland road between the refuge and the lower mountain pass: a rutted cart road through flat wet fields and hedgerows, milestones, a broken cart tipped in a roadside ditch (three winters old, wheels grown over with moss), the distant blue line of the mountains. Palette: pale field green, mud, slate sky, hedge dark green. Focal: the old cart. Mood: a road people keep walking.

### kiln_town (cs_rikka_ember_2 "Procession for the Missing")
Rikka's home: a potters' and kiln-workers' town on a clay hillside, beehive kilns with smoking chimneys, drying racks of unfired pots, a narrow main street strung with cords of red and ochre tassels for the procession, sparks rising from a kiln mouth at dusk. Palette: terracotta, soot black, ochre, ember orange sparks. Focal: the tassel-hung street. Mood: grief turned into noise and warmth.

### salt_road_coast (event_guild_salt_fever 2–3)
The salt villages on the southern coast road: whitewashed low houses with blue-grey shutters, evaporation pans shining in rows, a small stone harbour with fishing boats on the mud at low tide, a bakery with bread in the window, fever-house doors marked with a chalked circle (not crimson). Palette: salt white, grey-blue sea, weathered teal, sand. Focal: the harbour and the pans. Mood: poor, bright, stubborn.

### highland_ridge (event_sun_eclipse_day 2 "The Breath")
An open grassy ridge in the eastern highlands above a valley of scattered villages, a row of old wayside owing-bells on wooden posts along the ridge path, noon light dimmed by the eclipse (a thin crimson ring in the sky, small, the only crimson), long shadows at midday, wind in the grass. Palette: grey-green grass, stone, dim gold light, one thin crimson ring. Focal: the line of bells. Mood: the whole valley holding its breath.

### highland_village (event_sun_eclipse_day 3 "Tokens")
A small highland village square during the eclipse festival: stone houses with slate roofs, a village table where people hand out stamped brass tokens, children with smoked-glass shards to watch the sky, paper sun-and-moon charms on strings. Palette: slate, warm brass, festival paper colours muted by the dim light. Focal: the token table. Mood: superstition as a way of being remembered.
