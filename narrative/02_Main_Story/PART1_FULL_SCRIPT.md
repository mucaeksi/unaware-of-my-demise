## Chapter 1 — Whispering Ash Forest

*Where the trees still remember the names we carve.*

### opening_sad_scene — A Letter the Rain Could Not Keep

> *Black screen. Rain ticks on an abandoned roadside shrine. Fadyme, alone for one breath, presses a blood-marked glove to a letter and writes by candlelight. She folds the page before Mahmut enters. A distant bell rings once; ash drifts upward instead of down.*

**Fadyme (thought):** *If I wake before the bell tomorrow, I will call it luck. If I do not... Mahmut must never read this page.*  
**Fadyme (thought):** *One month, perhaps less. The old physician wrote it in Mother's prayer book. The ink does not tremble. I do.*  
**Mahmut:** You missed supper. I saved the part that did not burn.  
**Fadyme:** Then it is a feast. I am perfectly fine.  
**Mahmut:** Your hand is shaking.  
**Fadyme:** The cold has found my fingers. It has not found the rest of me.  
**Narration:** She hides the red stain beneath her cuff. He offers his cloak. She takes it, and lets him believe the warmth helped.  
**Yseldra:** Princess. The road ends at the altar. Give me the last hour before it takes you.  
**Mahmut:** You will not take her anywhere.  
**Yseldra:** I came to stop the bell, not to kill its prisoner. You cannot guard a door that has already chosen its offering.  
**Mahmut:** Then it can choose someone who is not under my shield.  
**Fadyme:** Do you know her?  
**Mahmut:** No.  
**Fadyme (thought):** *I know the bell she carries. And I know what it means when it rings for me.*  
**Yseldra:** When the names begin to vanish, remember: I warned you.  
**Narration:** Yseldra leaves a seven-notched gray seal on Mahmut’s shield. Fadyme turns away before he can see that she is crying.  

### 1-1 — The Road That Whispers

> *At gray dawn, ash leaves whisper the names carved into them. Mahmut hears only wind; Fadyme hears one syllable spoken in her mother’s voice.*

**— Before battle —**

**Mahmut:** Three wolves. Stay behind my shield.  
**Fadyme:** You say that as if I have ever listened.  
**Fadyme (thought):** *The bell has no clapper. Why can I still hear it?*  

**— After battle —**

**Mahmut:** The last one ran when the bell stopped.  
**Fadyme:** It did not stop. It only learned to whisper.  
**Fadyme (thought):** *One step. Then another. The road does not need to know how many it costs.*  

> *A guided two-hero prologue encounter is the only exception to the normal five-unit party rule. No Ration is spent.*

### 1-2 — No Bell in the Trees

> *At a collapsed wayshrine, Brin braces a fallen beam while Bellbound Dead scratch at the woodcutters’ shelter. Nixie has been scouting the road; Kera's herb cart is overturned beside the shrine. All three join the guided five-person battle as story loans.*

**— Before battle —**

**Brin:** If I move, the roof comes down. If I stay, they reach the children.  
**Mahmut:** You do not have to hold it alone.  
**Fadyme:** Three hands for the beam and three swords for the dead. We can manage both.  
**Nixie:** The tracks circle the shrine. We are being pushed toward the road, not away from it.  
**Kera:** My cart is full of smoke vials. If someone can throw straight, I can make that pack of corpses regret gathering.  

**— After battle —**

**Brin:** The woodcutters are safe. I will walk with you until the next bell is silent.  
**Mahmut:** That is farther than the next shrine.  
**Brin:** Then try to keep up.  
**Nixie:** I will mark the next stretch before the wolves can double back. You can borrow my bow until we reach the old cart road.  
**Kera:** And I am going to retrieve my bottles before they become somebody else's research.  
**Narration:** Brin joins permanently as a story recruit. Nixie and Kera remain as temporary story loans for the next stretch. The shrine keeper gives the party a First Oath Invitation.  

### ch1_first_oath — The Shrine Keeper's Register

> *Inside the wayshrine, the old keeper opens a register of oaths. Brin stands guard at the door.*

**Shrine Keeper:** You saved the woodcutters' children. The road owes you a companion.  
**Mahmut:** We did not ask for payment.  
**Shrine Keeper:** It is not payment. It is an oath. Travelers write here that they will walk for someone else when called.  
**Fadyme:** And they come? Just like that?  
**Shrine Keeper:** The oath binds their road to yours before your paths truly cross. You may meet them later as strangers. The oath remembers even when they do not.  
**Fadyme (thought):** *A promise that remembers people who forget. I should like one of those.*  
**Shrine Keeper:** Choose with care. An invitation can only be written once.  

### 1-3 — A Cart of Empty Bottles

> *Glass vials roll down a muddy slope. Kera is defending her overturned cart with a brass hand mortar and a look of personal offense.*

**— Before battle —**

**Kera:** Those bottles were sorted by volatility. You have turned them into a choir.  
**Mahmut:** We can help you gather them after the dead are gone.  
**Kera:** Good. Try not to step on the red ones. Or the blue ones. Or anything that smells like almonds.  

**— After battle —**

**Kera:** Take this route map. The Red Vial Guild owes me a favor, and now it owes you one too.  
**Fadyme:** You are coming with us?  
**Kera:** No. I am going ahead to warn the Guild. This is not concern. It is field research.  

> *Kera fills the fifth battle place as a guest unless the player already owns her. Guest presence never adds a second copy to the party.*

### 1-4 — The Map’s Little Lie

> *A twig snaps above the road. Nixie drops from a low branch, bow already drawn at the shadow behind the party.*

**— Before battle —**

**Nixie:** Do not follow the map. The map is lying. The wolves learned the road from it.  
**Mahmut:** And you learned that by climbing a tree?  
**Nixie:** I learned it by being chased up one. There is a difference.  

**— After battle —**

**Nixie:** I was going to Oakhaven anyway. I will take the rear watch.  
**Fadyme:** You do not have to stay with us.  
**Nixie:** I know. That is why I can choose to.  
**Narration:** Nixie joins permanently. The company now has five owned heroes: Mahmut, Fadyme, Brin, Nixie and the companion chosen by invitation.  

### 1-5 — The Ash-Maw

> *A dead wolf rises from the shrine ditch with a cracked bell fused through its jaw. The air goes silent around it.*

**— Before battle —**

**Nixie:** That is not a wolf. That is what the bell left wearing one.  
**Mahmut:** Front line, hold. Rear line, wait for the jaw to open.  
**Fadyme:** When it opens, strike the clapper. We only get one clean chance.  

**— After battle —**

**Brin:** The bell stopped. The trees are still whispering.  
**Fadyme:** They will forget the sound by morning.  
**Mahmut:** Your hand is freezing.  
**Fadyme:** Rain does that. I am perfectly fine.  
**Fadyme (thought):** *My fingertips have gone pale. I will tell him after the next town.*  

> *The boss has a visible two-phase transition; intent and target markers remain legible at x1, x2 or x3.*

### ch1_camp — Ash That Falls Upward

> *Night after the Ash-Maw. The company camps at the shrine. Far off, a cracked handbell rings once; the ash above the fire drifts up instead of down.*

**Brin:** The trees stopped whispering.  
**Nixie:** No. Listen. They are whispering someone else's name now.  
*Beyond the firelight, a single cracked bell rang, then went still.*  
**Mahmut:** Her.  
**Fadyme:** She is not coming closer. She wants us to know she is there.  
**Fadyme (thought):** *The widow's bell. It rang like that the night Mother's portrait came down.*  
**Mahmut:** Sleep. I will watch.  
**Fadyme:** You always say that. One day I will watch, and you will sleep.  


## Chapter 2 — The Ruined Cathedral of Saint Luthos

*A bell with no rope. A book with no page.*

### ch2_road — The Tower Across the Fields

> *Morning. Beyond the last ash trees, the bell tower of Saint Luthos rises over flooded fields. It has no rope.*

**Nixie:** Cathedral by noon if the fields do not eat our boots.  
**Fadyme:** I prayed there once. I was small enough to hide under the pews.  
**Mahmut:** You never told me.  
**Fadyme:** I am telling you now. See? I tell you things.  
**Fadyme (thought):** *Small things. Only small things.*  

### 2-1 — Rain in the Nave

> *Rain falls through the broken rose window. Fadyme stops at the nave threshold when an unstrung bell answers from above.*

**— Before battle —**

**Brin:** The forest road is full of people running from the bells. I will walk them to the lowland shrine. Keep the next bell quiet until I catch up.  
**Aveline:** Pilgrims first. Questions after. The clerics sealed the east aisle.  
**Mahmut:** We will open it.  
**Fadyme:** That bell has no rope.  

**— After battle —**

**Aveline:** The sound is coming from inside the stone.  
**Fadyme:** Then the stone remembers what we have forgotten.  
**Fadyme (thought):** *It remembers my name.*  

### 2-2 — The Choir Without Breath

> *Two Choir Knights sing without moving their mouths. A pale ward passes from shield to shield.*

**— Before battle —**

**Aveline:** Their ward is shared. Strike the bond, not the armor.  
**Mahmut:** Can you get the pilgrims through?  
**Aveline:** I can get them out. You can make the passage safe.  

**— After battle —**

**Aveline:** I will stay until every candle in the refuge is lit. Then I will follow the records.  
**Fadyme:** You should not have to choose between people and the truth.  
**Aveline:** Someone always has to choose. Let us make sure it is not the children.  

### 2-3 — The Guest Roll

> *Mirel unrolls a guest register under a brass reading lens. One line has been scraped so deeply that the parchment is nearly split.*

**— Before battle —**

**Mirel:** This register lists every royal visitor since the old king was crowned. One name was removed with a knife.  
**Fadyme:** Perhaps the ink had spoiled.  
**Mirel:** Ink fades. This was erased by someone afraid it would be read.  
**Mirel:** And the cut is fresh. Last winter, not years ago. Someone is still erasing this name.  

**— After battle —**

**Mirel:** There is a gap exactly where the heir’s name should be.  
**Mahmut:** You know who belongs there.  
**Fadyme:** I know who they wanted the world to forget.  
**Fadyme (thought):** *And the world is already doing it.*  

### 2-4 — A Page Beneath the Font

> *Aveline lifts a waterlogged page from beneath the font. Mirel holds its edges together while candlelight reveals one surviving sentence.*

**— Before battle —**

**Aveline:** A life held against the dark. That is the rite’s promise.  
**Mirel:** The sentence continues on a page someone carried away.  
**Mahmut:** Who wrote the promise?  

**— After battle —**

**Fadyme:** The person who wrote it may have thought it was mercy.  
**Mahmut:** Mercy does not need a missing page.  
**Fadyme:** No. But people do.  

### 2-5 — The Bell-Widow’s Verdict

> *At the archive door, Yseldra waits beneath the bell tower. Seven wax seals hang from her chain, each cut through the center.*

**— Before battle —**

**Yseldra:** You found the record. You still do not know what it asks of her.  
**Mahmut:** Then tell us.  
**Yseldra:** The altar does not cure. It keeps a wound open until the kingdom can be put back inside it.  
**Fadyme:** You came to take me away.  
**Yseldra:** I came to stop the next sacrifice. I will not pretend that makes my choice yours.  
**Yseldra:** And you, Sir Mahmut. You told her you did not know me.  
**Fadyme:** Mahmut?  
**Mahmut:** Not now.  

**— After battle —**

**Mahmut:** She escaped.  
**Mirel:** She could have killed us. She chose the bell tower instead.  
**Yseldra (distant):** Ask who keeps the hour running.  
**Nixie:** Oakhaven is marked on the map. I will warn them before the soldiers arrive.  
**Fadyme (thought):** *She knows I am dying. That does not mean I can let Mahmut know.*  
**Fadyme (thought):** *He lied to me, gently. So we are both keeping something.*  

> *Yseldra retreats alive; the scene contains no execution or kill animation. Nixie departs to warn Oakhaven.*

### ch2_camp — What We Did Not Say

> *The cathedral refuge after the Bell-Widow's escape. Fadyme finds Mahmut on the tower stair.*

**Fadyme:** You knew her.  
**Mahmut:** Yes.  
**Fadyme:** You said no. In the rain. You said it very fast.  
**Mahmut:** I thought if you did not know her, she could not frighten you.  
**Fadyme:** That is a very foolish reason.  
**Fadyme (thought):** *And it is mine too. I cannot scold him without scolding myself.*  
**Fadyme:** Tell me the rest when you can. I will not ask tonight.  
**Mahmut:** That is a kinder bargain than I deserve.  


## Chapter 3 — Oakhaven, the Red Harvest

*Every name board blank. Every door still standing.*

### ch3_road — Orchard Country

> *Three days of orchard roads. Fadyme walks slower on the hills; Mahmut adjusts his pace without comment.*

**Mahmut:** We will stop at the next wall.  
**Fadyme:** You stopped at the last wall. And the one before.  
**Mahmut:** They were good walls.  
**Fadyme:** You are pacing yourself for me.  
**Mahmut:** I am pacing myself for the walls.  
*She laughed, and then coughed, and turned her face into her scarf before he could see.*  

### 3-1 — The Empty Orchard

> *Nixie leads the company beneath bare apple trees. The fruit is still on the branches, but every name board in the orchard is blank.*

**— Before battle —**

**Nixie:** I warned them. The names are gone anyway.  
**Mahmut:** Who took them?  
**Nixie:** The men with red glass on their weapons. They are looking for Fadyme.  

**— After battle —**

**Fadyme:** A village can survive losing a name.  
**Nixie:** A person should not have to prove they belong in their own home.  
**Mahmut:** Then we give the names back.  
**Fadyme (thought):** *Every year I hid, someone else paid my hours.*  

### 3-2 — A Smith’s Last Bell

> *Jori has wedged her compact forge shield beneath the gate. She is too small to hold the door alone, but refuses to step away. Kera arrives on the Guild road with her rebuilt cart.*

**— Before battle —**

**Jori:** If the gate falls, the forge is next. If the forge falls, they take the tools.  
**Kera:** You can replace tools. You cannot replace a village.  
**Jori:** I know. That is why I am still standing here.  

**— After battle —**

**Mahmut:** You held the gate long enough for everyone to run.  
**Jori:** I made the shield because I was tired of doors being the only thing between us and danger.  
**Fadyme:** Then walk with us. A shield belongs where people need it.  

### 3-3 — The Well Runs Red

> *A red-vial mercenary tips a flask into the village well. Fadyme raises her silver bell focus; the spell takes more from her than she lets anyone see.*

**— Before battle —**

**Mahmut:** Fadyme, take the left side. I have the well.  
**Fadyme:** I can reach both.  
**Jori:** Your hand is bleeding.  
**Fadyme:** It is the mercenary’s blood. Keep the bucket moving.  

**— After battle —**

**Mahmut:** There is red on my gauntlet.  
**Fadyme:** Then the enemy finally managed to hit you.  
**Fadyme (thought):** *I wiped my own blood on his glove. He must not count the drops.*  

### 3-4 — The Inn of Borrowed Names

> *Pippa sings the lost names back to frightened villagers while a bell charm trembles at her wrist.*

**— Before battle —**

**Pippa:** If the ledger forgets you, I will sing louder than the paper.  
**Jori:** People are smiling again. I thought the bell took that too.  
**Fadyme:** Not everything precious can be written down.  

**— After battle —**

**Pippa:** I am carrying the names to the refuge. Someone has to keep them safe.  
**Jori:** I will go with Mahmut and Fadyme. The village has its gate back.  
**Mahmut:** Then the road has room for one more.  
**Narration:** Jori joins permanently as a story recruit.  

### 3-5 — The Mercenary Captain

> *Captain Rusk raises a signal banner over the orchard road. A volley is already aimed at the well.*

**— Before battle —**

**Captain Rusk:** The order says the heir comes quietly. It says nothing about the village.  
**Mahmut:** Your order is written by cowards.  
**Captain Rusk:** It was sealed by someone the Crown buried years ago.  

**— After battle —**

**Kera:** Seven notches. Same wax as the seal on your shield. There are letters beneath it: C.V.  
**Mahmut:** Do those initials mean anything to you?  
**Kera:** Not yet. The Red Vial ledger may know.  
**Fadyme (thought):** *C.V. I saw those letters on my father’s last page.*  
**Fadyme:** We should find a healer before we open the order.  
**Mahmut:** Then I will carry it, sealed, until we do.  

### ch3_camp — The Sealed Order

> *Oakhaven's inn, after the captain falls. Mahmut sets Rusk's sealed order on the table between them. Nixie has stayed behind in the refuge with Oakhaven's Vale children.*

**Kera:** We should open it. Knowledge is the only antidote I trust.  
**Fadyme:** A healer first. Please.  
**Jori:** Why does a healer matter for paper?  
**Fadyme:** Because whatever it says, someone at the table may need one afterward.  
**Kera:** ...The Undercity has the best healers. And the Guild ledger.  
**Mahmut:** Then we go down.  


## Chapter 4 — The Subterranean Undercity

*Not a month now. Weeks, perhaps less.*

### ch4_road — The River City

> *The river city at dusk. Beneath its bridges, drainage mouths breathe cold air. Chalk arrows mark one of them. At the marked mouth, two Red Vial scouts sit bound back to back, alive, a gray seven-notched seal pressed on each collar.*

**Jori:** Someone marked that tunnel. Neat arrows. Harbor knots.  
**Kera:** The Undercity. Everything the city does not want to see goes down there. Including my Guild.  
**Fadyme:** And its healers.  
**Kera:** Gray wax on Guild scouts, and the scouts still breathing. Someone is hunting my Guild's hunters.  
**Mahmut:** Stay close below. The dark down there is not like the forest.  
**Fadyme:** I know. The forest at least whispered.  

### 4-1 — Stairs Below the Cistern

> *Odelia’s chalk arrows lead beneath the city. The water is still, but something moves against its reflection.*

**— Before battle —**

**Odelia:** Do not look for ripples. These things follow the shape of your shadow.  
**Mahmut:** How did you find the tunnel?  
**Odelia:** I followed the people who stopped coming back.  

**— After battle —**

**Odelia:** The nest is behind us. The Guild ward is ahead.  
**Fadyme:** You did not have to follow us down here.  
**Odelia:** I dislike unanswered tracks. Yours are the loudest.  

### 4-2 — The Red Vial Ward

> *Tilda and Iona have turned a storage room into a clinic. Kera sets the recovered ledger beside their medicine jars.*

**— Before battle —**

**Tilda:** We have one clean table and three patients. I can make it four if you stop bleeding on it.  
**Iona:** She means welcome. Sit down before you fall down.  
**Kera:** I brought the ledger. Do not let anyone spill medicine on it.  

**— After battle —**

**Tilda:** The poison is gone. Fear takes longer.  
**Iona:** We will reopen this ward after the Guild is settled.  
**Fadyme:** You have given the city a place to breathe.  
**Iona:** Then keep breathing while you are in it.  
**Tilda:** The captain's order you carried, Sir Knight. It does not ask for her. It asks for her blood, sealed and delivered.  
**Kera:** Then someone in my Guild wrote a recipe around a person.  

### 4-3 — The Measure of a Life

> *Clem unlocks a narrow archive room. Mahmut waits at the stairwell while Fadyme asks for a private reading.*

**— Before battle —**

**Clem:** You asked for a truth with no witnesses. I can give you one.  
**Undercity Alchemist:** Not a month now. Weeks, perhaps less, if the bell keeps calling.  
**Fadyme:** And if I stop answering?  
**Undercity Alchemist:** Then perhaps the bell will take the answer from someone else.  

**— After battle —**

**Clem:** He will ask what I told you.  
**Fadyme:** Tell him the elixir might work.  
**Clem:** That is a lie.  
**Fadyme:** It is a small one. Let me keep it.  
**Fadyme (thought):** *Mahmut has already given enough years to my name.*  

### 4-4 — The Elixir Lie

> *Fadyme returns with a sealed vial. Kera recognizes the mixture from the guild ledger and sees the label is false. Later, alone, Fadyme refolds the letter from the forest road and tucks it deeper into her mother's prayer book.*

**— Before battle —**

**Mahmut:** What did the alchemist say?  
**Fadyme:** That the elixir might work.  
**Kera:** The label is wrong. The recipe is incomplete.  
**Fadyme:** Then complete it. You are good at turning bad mixtures into useful ones.  
**Kera:** And those are Chain Cultists on the stair. They pray to an eclipse that never moves, and they pay the Guild in silver for glass.  

**— After battle —**

**Kera:** I will try. I will not promise a cure.  
**Mahmut:** That is enough for tonight.  
**Fadyme (thought):** *He accepted a hope because I asked him to. I will have to live with that.*  

### 4-5 — The Red Vial Matron

> *The Matron seals herself behind a glass ward and floods the chamber with corrosive vapor.*

**— Before battle —**

**Red Vial Matron:** The heir’s blood is not a cure. It is the last ingredient.  
**Kera:** I knew the Guild was using the wrong formula. I did not know it was using her.  
**Fadyme:** Break the flasks. Do not let her finish the sentence.  

**— After battle —**

**Kera:** I am staying to copy every page of the antidote archive.  
**Tilda:** Iona and I will reopen the clinic. The patients need a door that stays open.  
**Iona:** I will hang the lantern first. People should see the door before they need it.  
**Mahmut:** We will come back for you.  
**Kera:** Bring the missing formula. I will bring the rest.  
**Jori:** Oakhaven has no smith while I am down here. I am going home to hold the gate. Send word when something needs a door.  
**Fadyme (thought):** *Weeks. Perhaps less. The words do not become kinder when repeated.*  

> *Kera remains to preserve the archive; Tilda and Iona return to the clinic.*

### ch4_camp — Up Into the Light

> *They climb out of the Undercity at dawn. Fadyme stops on the last stair to feel the sun.*

**Mahmut:** You are smiling.  
**Fadyme:** The sun is warm. I had forgotten it was allowed to be.  
**Mahmut:** The elixir. Did Kera say when?  
**Fadyme:** Soon. She is very good at soon.  
**Fadyme (thought):** *Weeks. I will spend some of them standing in the sun. That much I can choose.*  
**Mahmut:** East, then. The altar road crosses the ravine.  


## Chapter 5 — The Bridge of the Forsaken

*One white strand. One order signed.*

### ch5_road — Fog in the Ravine

> *Four days east. Refugees move the other way, carrying carts and children.*

**Refugee:** Do not cross the old bridge. Soldiers on it. Crown colors, upside down.  
**Mahmut:** Whose soldiers?  
**Refugee:** A marshal's. They are looking for a woman with a bell pin.  
**Fadyme:** Then she had better keep her hood up.  
**Mahmut (thought):** *Crown colors, upside down. Oren always hated sloppy banners.*  

### 5-1 — A Banner in the Fog

> *A herald’s pennant appears through ravine mist. Marek recognizes the empty crest socket on Mahmut’s armor.*

**— Before battle —**

**Marek:** That socket used to carry a crown. The road has not forgotten.  
**Mahmut:** The road remembers a version of me that no longer exists.  
**Marek:** Good. Then it can remember this one too.  

**— After battle —**

**Marek:** The refugees are across. I will carry your warning to the next watch post.  
**Fadyme:** You sing as if the bridge is still standing.  
**Marek:** A road is not a stone span. It is whoever agrees to walk it.  

### 5-2 — The Gatekeeper’s Measure

> *Nara braces a narrow gate while two siege winches pull its chains tight.*

**— Before battle —**

**Nara:** The gate can hold for one minute. The winches can be stopped in ten seconds. Choose the faster danger.  
**Mahmut:** You hold the civilians. We take the winches.  
**Nara:** That is the right order. Do not make me repeat it.  

**— After battle —**

**Nara:** The civilians are clear. I will stay with the gate until the last cart crosses.  
**Fadyme:** You always put yourself between people and the fall.  
**Nara:** Someone has to be the first thing the stone hits.  

### 5-3 — A Knife Between Orders

> *Sable and Rikka slip into a signal tower while the party draws guards away from the bell rope.*

**— Before battle —**

**Rikka:** I can break the door. I can also break the whole wall. Which one do you want?  
**Sable:** The door. We are rescuing prisoners, not improving the view.  
**Rikka:** You make every plan sound less fun.  

**— After battle —**

**Sable:** This order names Fadyme. The seal belongs to the old Crown Guard.  
**Mahmut:** My commander signed it.  
**Fadyme:** Then we ask him why.  
**Sable:** If he answers honestly, I will be surprised.  

### 5-4 — The White Strand

> *Odelia warns the party about mines. A bell pulse crosses the bridge; one lock of Fadyme’s ash-pale hair turns stark white, whiter than bone.*

**— Before battle —**

**Odelia:** The stones are hollow under the third span. Do not run when the bell rings.  
**Fadyme:** I was not going to run.  
**Mahmut:** Your voice changed. Are you hurt?  

**— After battle —**

**Fadyme:** Only tired. The wind is colder here.  
**Mahmut:** You have been tired since the forest.  
**Fadyme:** Then I have been consistent.  
**Mahmut:** Consistent is not the same as well.  
*She does not answer. For the first time, he does not let the question go, and for the first time, she has nothing ready.*  
**Fadyme (thought):** *A white strand. One more thing the mirror will remember before he does.*  

### 5-5 — The Commander Who Remained

> *Marshal Oren waits at the far end of the bridge with a shield line and Mahmut’s old crest painted upside down.*

**— Before battle —**

**Oren:** I gave the order. I did not know the rite would hollow her out.  
**Mahmut:** You knew enough to send men after her.  
**Oren:** I thought the seal would save the kingdom without consuming its heir.  
**Fadyme:** You thought a promise was the same as a plan.  
**Oren:** Yseldra carried the order from the Inquisition. I signed it. That is the whole of my courage.  

**— After battle —**

**Oren:** The Ashen estate has the full schedule. Go before the bridge falls.  
**Mahmut:** You are coming with us.  
**Oren:** No. Someone must keep this span open. I will not fail the people twice.  
**Rikka:** My kiln company is guarding the refugee road. They need someone loud. I am extremely loud!  
**Fadyme (thought):** *The white strand is small. I can still hide something small.*  

> *Oren stays to hold the bridge; Marek and Nara escort survivors, Rikka returns to her kiln company, and Sable advances toward the estate.*

### ch5_camp — Watch Kept

> *Night on the far bank. Mahmut sits with his back to the bridge. Fadyme brings two cups of weak tea.*

**Fadyme:** You have not spoken since the span.  
**Mahmut:** He taught me to hold a shield. He signed the order that made me drop it.

# Appendix C — Codex and Lore Text

**The Law of Hours.** Every life holds counted hours. They can be taken, given, or owed, but never moved without a witness and consent.

**The Seven Bells.** House Veyr bound seven bells to count the hours of the living. They do not heal. They keep the ledger.

**Names as Witnesses.** An hour cannot be written without a name that remembers it. That is why Veyrhold carves names on trees and doors.

**Hollows.** Bodies left behind by unpaid hours. Their names are gone; their hours are not.

**Crownfall.** Six years ago the capital fell, the king died, and the chancellor was recorded dead. The Council of Dukes has ruled since.

**Vale Children.** Children with no registered family are written into road registers as "Vale". It is a common name, and a lonely one.

**The Seven-Notched Seal.** The mark of the Royal Inquisition, keepers of sealed records. Its seals still appear on orders no one admits writing.

**The Red Vial Guild.** Alchemists and physicians. One wing keeps antidotes; the other kept a ledger of people.

**Saint Luthos the Listener.** He taught that a bell is a prayer the whole village hears, and that names spoken aloud are witnesses for the living.

**The First Eclipse.** Generations ago the bells were first fixed with an heir's hours. The eclipse over the altar has not moved since.

**The Council of Dukes.** Seven seals on one uncut cord. They keep the kingdom running, and the throne empty.

**The Clockless Court.** A children's story about a court where no one ages. Most people think it is only a story.
