# The Ashen Lord (Lord Edric Aske) — Boss, Chapter 6

Approved 2026-09-30 (decision C38). Not playable. Boss `boss_ashen_lord_wraith` at 6-5. His name is **never spoken in Part I**: the Council erased it; the player knows him only as "the Ashen Lord".

## Who he was

- **Office:** Keeper of the Schedule of the Sevenfold Crown. From his estate in the eastern highlands, near the crater, he calculated when and in what order the bells would collect, and so he timed every Offering. "Ashen" is the estate's name for the ash that drifts down from the crater and the ash of spent hours; the house is **House Aske**.
- **What he knew:** better than anyone what the Crown did with Veyr daughters, because he wrote the dates. After the Silent Bell failed he scheduled the seven stand-in bearers.
- **What he did:** he took the eighth, the last daughter of the bell line, off the Schedule and hid her in his house ("I hid the bell-keeper's daughter from the Crown"). The king met her there; Fadyme was born in the estate nursery and lived there until she was seven.
- **Cael and the Schedule:** Cael built the Rewrite on the Crown's Schedule; the C.V.-signed handless-watch diagram in the estate ledger (6-4) is the change Cael brought him before Crownfall, under the Inquisition seal.

## How he died

After Crownfall Cael asked for the Schedule. Lord Aske locked himself in the archive with it and gave his last order: *no one comes in, no one goes out; the Schedule leaves this house in no one's hands.* He died there in the first winter after Crownfall. The Council's erasure order had already cut his name and his family's nameplates; with no name to witness him he could not cross, and he held on through the portraits: a **Wraith** (`lore/bestiary.md`).

## Why he fights

He is not cruel. Like every Hollow he repeats his last task: **hide them from the Crown.** The house "still gives orders"; he locks the servants below to keep them hidden and takes anyone who opens a door for the Crown. He woke fully about three weeks before 6-1, when the Schedule began to run toward the Altar (Fadyme took the road; the bells quickened). The archive that collapses at 6-4 is the room where he died.

## Lines (existing)

- "A house remembers its master. A kingdom remembers its heir."
- "I hid the bell-keeper's daughter from the Crown. I kept her face and lost her name." (Both halves are true: her name and his.)

## Visual

Noble of about sixty, dressed as in his own portrait; every portrait in the gallery wears his face over a cut nameplate; ash-grey, tarnished gold, cold glass teal (location brief). The mirror frame holds him. No skull, no gore.
