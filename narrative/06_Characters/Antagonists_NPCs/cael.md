# Lord Cael Veyr — Antagonist

See also `docs/VILLAIN_BIBLE_PART1.md` (authoritative for role and motive).

## Backstory (director pass 2026-09-28)

- **Identity:** Lord Cael Veyr, the Clockless Chancellor. 41 at Crownfall; current age uncertain. Not playable.
- **Origin:** Born into House Veyr, the bell-keepers. Older brother of Fadyme's mother. Veyr by birth but not of the bell line, which passes from mother to daughter (C16): the Ledger could never take him as its Last Bell. The only Veyr who chose court over bells: he became royal chancellor and keeper of the court chronometer, and removed its hands on the day he took office ("a chancellor should not be ruled by the hour").
- **His household:** A wife, **Liora**, and a son, **Aren**, who died in the throne wars two years before Crownfall, in a siege Cael had scheduled to the minute and lost by one hour.
- **The design (C20):** He designed **the Rewrite**. On Crownfall night he took the last unrecorded hour, **the Unwritten Hour**, out of the Ledger; with enough paid hours it can be rewritten at the Altar, returning Veyrhold to the minute before Crownfall. The payment is a Last Bell's hours, routed toward the Altar by **the Schedule**; the instrument is **the Hinge** (the Eclipse Construct), built into the stopped moment of the First Eclipse so the Altar counts backward. He wrote to the king about it; the king's last page carries his initials.
- **Hidden aim (SECRET until Part II, C20):** the minute before Crownfall is only the first turn. If the Hinge keeps counting back, it reaches the hour he lost the siege in which Liora and Aren died. He has told no one.
- **Crownfall:** The king refused his letter (it became the king's last page). After the king's death Cael, as chancellor, issued both orders under the Inquisition seal: seal a bearer, then deliver her to the Altar (C24). He stepped out of recorded time with the last unrecorded hour and was listed among the dead. Since then his name slips from memory; people remember "the chancellor", not Cael (rule 11, C26).
- **Now:** Keeps the Clockless Court. He believes one life is a fair price for a kingdom and calls the price temporary because the hour will be rewritten. He is gentle to Fadyme, and he has never once asked what she wants.

## Visual story

- **Look (for the new sheet):** slender adult man, dark hair, grey eyes; severe high-collared black court coat over **antique-gold scale panels**; one **empty signet socket** on the right hand; gloves with no insignia; a small watch on a signet chain with an **empty dial and no hands**. Face partly caught in fractured mirror reflections, never a mask.
- **Palette:** ink black, aged gold, cold white. Mark: open circle with a missing clock hand and one broken crown arc.
