# Corbin Locke — Tier C

**Identity:** 29 · male · Tide harpoon vanguard · 5★ limited (Oath Banner 7) · former Chief Sluice-Warden of Glassford's lower cisterns, now protector of the Undercity clinic.
**Desire:** Reopen the lowest cistern and wash the Red Vial Guild's glass wards clean. **Fear:** Drowning in dark water where no lantern keeper knows your name. **Link [NEW]:** He was the last person outside the Guild to see Odelia's brother, Tomas Pike, alive on the lower cistern stairs two winters ago, and kept the Guild from scrubbing Tomas's harbor-knot chalk arrow off the sluice wall (4-1). **Contradiction:** Looks like a rough dockside brawler who settles arguments with a gaff-hook, yet knows the water-pressure acoustics of every pipe beneath Glassford and speaks in a hush inside Tilda's clinic so he never wakes a feverish patient.
**Arc:** Guards the cistern descent with Odelia (4-1) and Tilda/Iona's clinic door (4-2) → floods the Matron's outer reagent sluice (4-5) → inspects the mined bridge piers with Odelia (5-4) and holds the flooded conduit approach at the Altar (7-4).
**Weapon — Low-Water Key:** heavy brass-barbed river harpoon tethered by wet anchor-chain to a notched iron sluice-wheel buckler strapped to his left forearm. "The river remembers its level, even when they brick the arch."
**Voice:** Deep, unhurried coastal bass-baritone; dry dockworker wit; protective older-brother steadiness toward Odelia. **Never says:** "The water took them."
**Companion story:** 1 *The Lowest Sluice* — the night the Red Vial ledger wing sealed the Glass Bell cistern. 2 *One Clean Table* — how Tilda and Iona pulled him from the spillway. 3 *The Chalk Arrow on the Lock* — what he tells Odelia about Tomas's last watch. 4 *High Water* — reopening the Undercity floodgates after 4-5.

## Backstory (director pass 2026-10-18)

- **Origin:** Born into a family of river lock-keepers in Glassford; learned to read water levels, stone foundations and drowned currents before he could read ink.
- **Sealing the cistern (Year −2):** Two winters before Part I, the Red Vial Matron ordered the lowest cistern drained and bricked off so the ledger wing could tap the Glass Bell's wards (`seven_bells.md`). When Corbin refused to surrender the master sluice-wheel and tried to flood the laboratory conduit, Guild mercenaries threw him into the lower spillway in chains.
- **The clinic:** Tilda Fen dragged him out of the drainage canal and stitched his torn shoulder on her single clean table; Iona Mist kept her lantern lit above the door and read his name aloud until his fever broke. Corbin bolted the broken iron sluice-wheel to his left forearm as a shield and has guarded their clinic door ever since.
- **When Part I begins:** He is holding the cistern stairs against Glass Vipers and keeping the Guild's collectors away from Tilda's nine patients.
