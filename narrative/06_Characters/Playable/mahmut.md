# Mahmut — Tier A

## 1. Identity
28 · male · Stone tank-bruiser · 5★ free start · former sworn palace guard, branded traitor by the Council of Dukes · "Sir Mahmut" only to those who knew him before.

## 2. Core psychology
- **Desire:** Keep Fadyme walking beside him.
- **Fear:** Arriving too late a second time; obeying the wrong order again.
- **Belief:** Protection is a debt you pay with your body.
- **False belief:** You can carry a truth for someone by hiding it from them.
- **Contradiction:** An "oathless" knight who lives more by his oath than anyone sworn.
- **Secret (Part I):** He knew Yseldra and saw her call Fadyme to the altar at Crownfall. Exposed at 2-5, confessed after 7-5.
- **Regret:** Letting Fadyme out of his sight during the Crownfall flight.

## 3. Motivation
- Thinks he wants: to break the mechanism and cure her.
- Actually needs: to let her choose, and to be chosen rather than obeyed.
- What blocks change: he measures love in shields.

## 4. Arc
- **Part I:** Reactive guardian → learns she is dying and that they both pretended. Ends facing Cael's offer.
- **Part II:** The "one hour" is his. His arc is the difference between giving and paying.

## 5. Relationships
Fadyme (devotion, mutual concealment) · Oren (betrayed father figure, unfinished) · Yseldra (the witness he lied about) · Marek (recognizes the old crest) · Brin, Nara, Jori (the other "walls"; he learns from Brin's 1-2 line he once gave).

## 6. Combat and weapon
Front-line redirect tank: *Broken Crest Guard* takes blows meant for the weakest ally — the story of his life in one skill.
**Approved look (art canon, `art/sheets/char-mahmut-uomd.png`):** dark messy hair, stubble, brown eyes, dark worn half-plate, tattered oxblood cloak. The Crown Guard star stays on his breastplate; the crown that sat above it was hammered off, leaving the empty socket Marek recognizes (5-1).

**The Crestless Oath [NEW lore]:** his Crown Guard sword; he filled the crest socket in its hilt with plain iron the night he hammered the crest from his armor. Weapon lore line: "An oath with the name filed off still cuts."

## 7. Voice
Short declaratives, dry humor, anger shown as silence. Uses "we" when afraid. **Never says:** "That is an order." · "I am sorry" lightly · anything grand about himself.

## 8. Foreshadowing / risks
- The "one hour that belongs to you" should connect to Crownfall night (the hour he chose to disobey) — decide before Part II.
- Risk: Mahmut becoming only a reaction to Fadyme. Give him choices in every Part II chapter.

## Backstory (director pass 2026-09-28)

- **Origin:** Born in the lower ward of Carrowgate, the capital, the son of a Crown Guard farrier. He grew up shoeing warhorses for knights who never learned his name, and learned early that the people who hold things up are rarely the ones who are thanked.
- **Into the Guard:** Entered the Crown Guard at fifteen. Marshal Oren noticed that he never broke formation to save himself, only to cover someone else, and made him a shield-bearer.
- **The heir's shield:** At nineteen he was assigned as escort to the king's recognized daughter, Fadyme, then thirteen and newly brought to court: the public declaration had made her a target (C23). For three years he walked two steps behind her through corridors that did not want her there. This is the "years" Fadyme says he has already given her name (4-3).
- **Crownfall (age 22):** He saw Yseldra call Fadyme's name at the bell altar beneath the Silent Bell and seal her. When Oren ordered him to escort the sealed heir to the First Eclipse Altar, he chose to disobey (C19). He got Fadyme out of the capital, lost her in the flight, and was branded a traitor by the Council of Dukes. He hammered the crown off his Guard star that same week.
- **Six lost years:** Four years as a sellsword and caravan guard under no name, searching every village register for hers. He found her two years ago in a hill village, with her plan for the altar already written (he took it for an escape plan; C33). He stayed because she asked, not because she needed him.
- **When Part I begins:** He believes the curse is a mechanism that can be broken, and that his job is to walk in front of it.

## Visual story

- **Why he looks like this:** The dark half-plate is his Guard armor, never replaced, repaired by village smiths in six different styles. The oxblood cloak was Guard red; he dyed it darker so it would not be recognized, and never mended the tears because each one is a road he walked looking for her. The star stays on his chest because the oath was never to the crown above it.
- **Stubble and tired eyes:** He sleeps last and wakes first. The sheet's "Early Days" state (tired, but steady) is his default.
- **Signature objects:** the repaired heater shield (Yseldra's seal is still pressed into its rim from the opening), the arming sword with the iron-filled crest socket.
