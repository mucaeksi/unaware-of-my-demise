# Jori Flint — Tier B

**Identity:** 20 · Ember guard · 3★ free story recruit · Oakhaven smith; family forged and repaired the altar chains.
**Desire:** Make things that protect people instead of doors. **Fear:** That her family's craft built a prison. **Contradiction:** Hot-tempered, deeply ashamed.
**Arc:** Holds the gate (3-2), leaves after 4-5 to guard Oakhaven, returns (7-1): "We made a cage and called it a bridge." Part II: forges the tool that can open, not break, a chain.
**Weapon — Furnace Door's Rivet:** hammer-shield with a rivet from the family's chain die. "Every door has a smith. Choose which side you stand on."
**Voice:** Blunt, quick temper, commands to hide fear. **Never says:** "It's just a job."
**Companion story:** 1 *Forge Gate* — Oakhaven. 2 *Grandmother's Die* — the chain pattern. 3 *Harvest Bell* — the bell her family cast. 4 *A Key, Not a Hammer*.

## Backstory (director pass 2026-09-28)

- **Origin:** The Flint forge in Oakhaven has made gates, bridges and chains for generations; its grandmother's die stamps every link.
- **Why shields:** At fourteen she watched the forge gate break during a raid and decided doors were not enough. She began making small shields that "go where the person goes".
- **When Part I begins:** She is wedging her shield under Oakhaven's forge gate, too small to hold it and refusing to step away.

## Visual story

- **Look:** petite; short ginger hair tied up with a strip of leather, soot on her nose, burn freckles on her forearms; thick leather apron, arm guards; round short shield and small hammer.
- **Palette:** soot black, iron, copper (ginger hair is the accent). Silhouette: shield first, hammer cocked.
