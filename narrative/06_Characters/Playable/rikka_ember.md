# Rikka Ember — Tier C

**Identity:** 18 · Ember bruiser DPS · 4★ · festival spear-guard of a kiln company.
**Desire:** Be remembered as the one who made people brave enough to laugh. **Fear:** Silence — the bells taught her that quiet means something is wrong. **Belief:** Noise scares Hollows. **Contradiction:** Loud to hide how carefully she counts exits.
**Folk lore [NEW]:** Kiln companies ring small bell tassels in processions to "confuse the counting"; it does nothing to the Law of Hours, but people believe it.
**Weapon — Procession's First Spark:** spear with a bell tassel; first lit at her first procession. "Loud enough to be heard by the living."
**Voice:** Exclamations, boasts, then a sudden steady breath in combat. **Never says:** "Be quiet."
**Companion story:** 1 *Kiln Company* — home. 2 *The Barricade Was My Idea* — 7-2 from her side. 3 *Procession for the Missing* — a festival for names. 4 *Quiet* — the one time she chooses silence.

## Backstory (director pass 2026-09-28)

- **Origin:** Youngest daughter of a kiln-town potter family in the eastern hills. Kiln companies are part guild, part festival troupe, part militia.
- **Procession guard:** At fifteen she carried the spear at her first bell procession and set the banner on fire. The town cheered. She has been the loudest person at every procession since.
- **Why loud:** When she was twelve, the town went silent during a Hollow night and her grandmother did not wake. Rikka decided silence was the enemy.
- **When Part I begins:** Her kiln company is guarding bridge refugees; she volunteers to break doors for Sable.

## Visual story

- **Look:** bright red hair in two thick braids, soot on her cheeks, wide grin; closed red lamellar festival armor with gold cord; spear with a small bell tassel.
- **Palette:** ember red, brick, festival gold (not Kera's orange-brown). Silhouette: forward lunge, braids flying.
