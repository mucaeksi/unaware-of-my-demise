# Sable Wren — Tier C

**Identity:** 21 · Veil assassin DPS · 5★ limited (Oath Banner 2) · courier-infiltrator.
**Desire:** To stop carrying other people's secrets. **Fear:** Learning what she delivered. **Secret [NEW]:** As a teenage courier she carried sealed orders across the eastern road; one of them, a month after Crownfall, was the Council's first commission sending Oren after the heir (C27, C36). The Crownfall order itself was carried by Yseldra. **Contradiction:** Opens doors for others, never walks through first.
**Arc:** Finds Oren's order (5-3), goes to the estate alone, guides everyone out with lanterns (7-4: "nobody gets lost in the dark tonight").
**Weapon — Quietus at the Broken Bell:** twin blades and a severed bell rope; she cut the rope of the Forsaken Bell's signal tower (5-3). "Silence is a message too."
**Voice:** Near-whisper, dry, economical. **Never says:** a name before she is outside ("No names until we are outside").
**Companion story:** 1 *Sealed Delivery* — an old courier route. 2 *Wax* — recognizing her own delivery in Rusk's order chain. 3 *The Servants' Door* — how she found the estate servants. 4 *Lanterns* — she tells one person the whole truth.

## Backstory (director pass 2026-09-28)

- **Origin:** Raised in the **Wren Post**, a courier house in the eastern road towns that took in quick, quiet orphans. Every Wren courier carries a wax identity seal instead of a surname.
- **Sealed deliveries:** From twelve she ran sealed orders; seven-notch orders paid double. At fifteen, the month after Crownfall, she carried the Council's first commission to Oren: the order that sent him after the heir (C36).
- **Leaving the Post:** At eighteen a delivery she carried ended in a village burning. She broke her courier seal and began working alone, opening doors for people inside instead of carrying messages to the people outside.
- **When Part I begins:** She is infiltrating Crown Guard supply towers on the eastern bridge, looking for orders like the ones she used to carry.
