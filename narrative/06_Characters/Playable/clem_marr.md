# Clem Marr — Tier B

**Identity:** 19 · Veil debuffer · 3★ standard · grave-register decoder.
**Desire:** Every dead person has a name. **Fear:** Being the reason a living person is forgotten. **Contradiction:** Cheerful about graves, anxious about lies.
**Mystery role:** First witness of Fadyme's lie ("That is a lie." 4-3). Part II: Mahmut's "you knew too" confrontation.
**Weapon — The Name Beneath Stone:** ritual lantern that shows chalk names under moss. "Most names are only hiding."
**Voice:** Curious, macabre anecdotes, sudden bluntness. **Never says:** "Forget it."
**Companion story:** 1 *Rubbings* — her work. 2 *Witness* — carrying Fadyme's secret. 3 *The Unmarked Row* — Hollows' graves with no names. 4 *Keep the Names* — choosing to speak.

## Backstory (director pass 2026-09-28)

- **Origin:** Granddaughter of a gravedigger in Glassford, the river city. She learned to read from headstones.
- **Grave-register work:** From fifteen she deciphered moss-covered stones for families who had lost records after Crownfall. The Undercity alchemists hired her to read their oldest archive rooms.
- **When Part I begins:** She knows the Undercity's locked rooms better than its wardens, which is why Fadyme asks her for a truth "with no witnesses".

## Visual story

- **Look:** petite, round-faced; messy mouse-brown bob dusted with chalk; heather-grey work jacket, mustard scarf, chalk and brushes in every pocket; ritual lantern with symbol cut-outs.
- **Palette:** heather grey, chalk white, mustard. Silhouette: kneeling at a stone, lantern raised.
