# Hana Birch — Tier C

**Identity:** 18 · Stone counter DPS · 3★ standard · forest trap warden.
**Desire:** Prove shaking hands can still open doors (6-1). **Fear:** Speaking up. **Burden (now canon):** She saw Fadyme's arm at 6-4 and promised silence. **Contradiction:** Timid in words, fearless in action.
**Weapon — Stillbark Snare:** hatchet and small trap from her forest post. "Stillness is also a strike."
**Voice:** Short, hesitant, then firm. **Never says:** "It's not my business."
**Companion story:** 1 *Children Under the Stair* — the estate rescue. 2 *The Promise* — keeping Fadyme's secret. 3 *Lantern Path* — leading the evacuees at 7-4. 4 *Say It* — when she chooses to speak.

## Backstory (director pass 2026-09-28)

- **Origin:** Daughter of the Ashen estate's gamekeeper; grew up in the lodge at the edge of the birch woods.
- **The estate:** After the Ashen Lord's death the house kept "giving orders". Her father refused to hunt for it and left; Hana stayed to watch the servants' children.
- **When Part I begins:** She is setting snares in the birch woods and sneaking food to the servants locked below.

## Visual story

- **Look:** petite; honey-brown short hair under a birch-bark-white bandana; quilted rust-brown clothes, a shield plate strapped above the knee, rope coils, a small bear trap and a short hatchet.
- **Palette:** rust, birch white, moss. Silhouette: crouched beside a snare, very still.
