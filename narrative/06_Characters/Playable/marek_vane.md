# Marek Vane — Tier C

**Identity:** 30 · Gale control buffer · 4★ · road herald, former border guard.
**Desire:** Keep roads walkable ("A road is whoever agrees to walk it"). **Fear:** Marching without a reason. **Past [NEW]:** He carried the news of Crownfall along the western border; he recognized Mahmut's stripped crest because he announced the treason decree himself. **Contradiction:** A herald who distrusts proclamations.
**Weapon — March Without a Banner:** spear-horn; he removed its herald pennant after Crownfall. "The road needs rhythm more than colors."
**Voice:** Calm tenor, counts rhythm, aphorisms. **Never says:** "By order of the Council."
**Companion story:** 1 *Herald* — the decree he read. 2 *Refugee Measure* — a crossing. 3 *Retraction* — he announces Mahmut's truth in one village. 4 *No Banner* — why he walks.

## Backstory (director pass 2026-09-28)

- **Origin:** Son of a western border-post sergeant; served as a border guard until twenty, then as a Crown herald.
- **The decree:** After Crownfall he read Mahmut's treason decree aloud in eleven villages. He stopped carrying paper the same year and began walking with refugees instead.
- **When Part I begins:** He is herding refugees across the eastern bridge with a counting song.

## Visual story

- **Look:** tall; long dark-brown hair tied back, short beard, calm grey eyes; long road cloak, closed dark leather coat, a viola across his back, spear-horn with its pennant removed.
- **Palette:** road brown, faded blue, brass. Silhouette: walking, not standing; instrument and spear both visible.
