# Mirel Quill — Tier B

**Identity:** 25 · Dawn buffer · 5★ limited (Oath Banner 4) · former court scribe, now archivist.
**Desire:** Records that tell the truth. **Fear:** That her own hand erased people. **Secret (now canon):** She copied the erasure orders for the Veyr name as a court scribe (6-2). **Contradiction:** Believes ink is sacred; has betrayed it.
**Arc:** Finds the cut register (2-3) → recognizes her hand (6-2) → writes Fadyme's name back herself (7-3). Part II: restores other erased names; the Council wants her silenced.
**Weapon — The Unwritten Ledger:** brass astrolabe folio and stylus dagger; its last page is always blank. "Some pages wait for the right hand."
**Voice:** Precise, wry, footnotes in speech. **Never says:** "It doesn't matter what it says."
**Companion story:** 1 *Marginalia* — her old scriptorium. 2 *Copyist* — the orders she copied. 3 *Corrected Record* — a public correction that costs her. 4 *Decree of the Unfallen* — a list of names read aloud.

## Backstory (director pass 2026-09-28)

- **Origin:** Daughter of a court copyist; entered the palace scriptorium at fourteen.
- **The erasures:** At nineteen, the month after Crownfall, she copied forty erasure orders for the Council, including the Veyr name. She timed herself and was proud of her speed.
- **Leaving court:** At twenty-two she found one of the names she had erased carved on a grave. She resigned and took work as archivist for Saint Luthos, telling no one why.
- **When Part I begins:** She is cataloguing the cathedral's water-damaged registers and finds a cut she recognizes as a court hand.

- **Palette:** bottle green, ink black, brass. Silhouette: astrolabe folio open in one hand, stylus raised like a baton.
