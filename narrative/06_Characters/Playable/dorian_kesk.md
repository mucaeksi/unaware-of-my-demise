# Dorian Kesk — Tier B

**Identity:** 26 · male · Veil cipher-debuffer DPS · 4★ · former novice seal-cutter of the Royal Inquisition, now an Undercity pass-forger and cipher broker allied with Clem Marr and Sable Wren.
**Desire:** Counterfeit enough safe passes that no border guard can trap a nameless refugee again. **Fear:** That Yseldra — or whoever still holds the Chancellor's private die — will find the stolen Inquisition seal matrix in his coat. **Secret [NEW]:** On Crownfall night, as a twenty-year-old novice in the Inquisition chancery, he mixed the gray wax for the two orders signed `C.V.` underneath (`canon.md` F-022). When the Inquisition broke, he stole a seven-notched seal matrix and fled to Glassford's Undercity. **Contradiction:** Talks like a cynical underworld broker who claims everything has a fee, yet charges refugees "one copper or a passable lie" for forged safe-conducts that would get him hanged.
**Arc:** Guides Clem and Mahmut past the Undercity wardens (4-3, 4-4) → meets Sable on the Bridge of the Forsaken to compare the wax on Rusk's and Oren's orders (5-3) → slips into the Ashen Estate servants' wing with Sable (6-1) and identifies the chancery wax on the 6-4 ledger margin. Part II: his stolen matrix is the only tool that can open sealed Inquisition vaults in Carrowgate.
**Weapon — Counterfeit Verdict:** smoked-glass and blackened-steel dueling rapier paired with a heated brass wax-cauterizer and seal-iron in his off-hand (`rapier_seal_iron`). "A real seal takes a life. Mine gets you past the gate."
**Voice:** Smooth, quick-witted tenor; chancery and legal terms twisted into dry street irony; drops all cynicism when speaking to foundlings or Vale children. **Never says:** "The seal is authentic."
**Companion story:** 1 *Gray Wax* — Crownfall night at the chancery table. 2 *One Copper or a Lie* — forging road passes above Tilda's clinic. 3 *The Seventh Notch* — comparing seal impressions with Sable Wren after 5-3. 4 *A Name They Cannot File* — why he helps Clem keep her grave-book.

## Backstory (director pass 2026-10-18)

- **Origin:** Apprenticed at twelve to a court engraver in Carrowgate before being requisitioned by the Royal Inquisition's lower chancery to cut wax matrices and inspect sealed dispatches.
- **Crownfall (age 20):** Heated the gray wax for the last two orders issued under the Inquisition seal on Crownfall night — the sealing order and the delivery order carrying the initials `C.V.` beneath the wax. He saw Yseldra Vale take the second order into the rain.
- **The Undercity:** Fled south with a seven-notched brass matrix sewn into his coat lining. In Glassford's Undercity he became the quiet broker who forges Ducal travel passes for erased families and hires Clem Marr to verify names from old headstones so his forged papers use real ancestral lines.
- **When Part I begins:** He is watching the canal bridges outside the Red Vial ward, stalling Guild wardens with forged inspection warrants while Clem leads Fadyme to the old alchemist's locked room (4-3).
