# Kera Voss — Tier B

**Identity:** 20 · Ember alchemist DPS · 4★ · Red Vial Guild, archive wing.
**Desire:** Prove a cure can exist without a victim. **Fear:** That her work has already helped kill someone. **False belief:** Every problem is a formula. **Contradiction:** Says "this is not concern" while caring loudly.
**Secret [NEW]:** As an apprentice she sold blood-sample analyses to the ledger wing without asking what they were for. The Matron's line at 4-5 is personal.
**Arc:** Part I: suspicion → learns the Guild is using Fadyme → stays to guard the archive. Part II: her unfinished formula is the scientific rival to Cael's offer; she must decide whether an imperfect cure is worth the risk.
**Relationships:** Fadyme (keeps her lie, resents it) · Tilda/Iona (colleagues) · Mahmut (respects, lies to him by silence).
**Weapon — Redoubt's Last Vial [NEW lore]:** the brass mortar of her first master, who was expelled for refusing the ledger wing. "Measured twice, poured once, regretted never."
**Voice:** Fast, sharp endings, lists, technical words used as insults. **Never says:** "I don't know" without "yet".
**Companion story:** 1 *Sorted by Volatility* — rebuilding her cart and her notes. 2 *The Sample Book* — finds her old analyses in the ledger. 3 *Honest Labels* — must publicly expose a Guild patron. 4 *Formula, Incomplete* — sets the Part II research hook.

## Backstory (director pass 2026-09-28)

- **Origin:** Born in Glassford, the river city above the Undercity, daughter of a glassblower who supplied the Red Vial Guild. She could read a recipe before she could read a psalm.
- **Apprenticeship:** At twelve she was taken on by **Master Hesper Doyle**, an archive-wing alchemist famous for refusing anything she could not label. At fourteen, a failed ember tincture blew out Hesper's workshop window and bleached Kera's hair to its present ash-silver overnight; she decided it looked scholarly and has told three different stories about it since.
- **The sample book:** At seventeen, eager to prove herself, she analyzed blood samples for the ledger wing without asking who they came from (companion story 2). One page reads "heir line, maternal: stable, remarkable".
- **Hesper's exile:** The same winter, Hesper refused to work for the ledger wing and was expelled. She left Kera her wide field hat and brass mortar and told her: "Measure twice. Then ask who is paying."
- **When Part I begins:** Kera travels as a field alchemist with a cart of sorted bottles, officially researching Hollow ash, privately checking whether the Guild's orders smell wrong. She is right.

## Visual story

- **Why she looks like this:** The wide-brimmed hat is Hesper's field hat, built to keep sparks off the face; Kera added the ember-lens goggles herself, and they are the only thing she will admit to being sentimental about. Ash-silver hair from the explosion.
- **Coat, apron, flask belt:** archive-wing field kit. The cream apron is always clean except for one scorch mark she will not wash out.
- **Distinctness note:** Her pale hair is explained by an accident, not by Veyr blood. Future art must keep her silhouette dominated by the hat, not the hair, so she never reads as a Fadyme look-alike.
