# Iona Mist — Tier C

**Identity:** 24 · Tide healer · 4★ · river lantern keeper.
**Desire:** Bring the lost home, living or dead. **Fear:** A name with no one to call it. **Differs from Tilda:** Iona listens and guides; she treats memory and grief as wounds.
**Role [NEW]:** River lantern keepers read names aloud as boats pass so the drowned are witnessed; the practice matches the Law of Hours.
**Weapon — Safe Harbor Lantern:** tidal censer and glass lantern. "No one crosses the fog alone."
**Voice:** Calm, analytic, gentle directness. **Never says:** "They are gone."
**Companion story:** 1 *Lantern Watch* — the river. 2 *Names on the Water* — reading the lost. 3 *Fog Ward* — the Altar infirmary. 4 *Harbor* — she offers to read Fadyme's name each night.

## Backstory (director pass 2026-09-28)

- **Origin:** Daughter of river lantern keepers on the fog-bound river below the city.
- **Names on the water:** Lantern keepers read the names of the drowned aloud as boats pass so they are witnessed. Iona has done it since she was ten.
- **Undercity:** At twenty-two she followed the river's lost into the Undercity and met Tilda.
- **When Part I begins:** She keeps the clinic's lantern lit above its door.

## Visual story

- **Look:** olive skin, long straight black hair with a river-blue sheen; deep navy oilskin raincoat, hood down; glass river lantern; staff veined with blue mist.
- **Palette:** deep navy, lantern amber, fog grey. Silhouette: lantern raised, head tilted, listening.
