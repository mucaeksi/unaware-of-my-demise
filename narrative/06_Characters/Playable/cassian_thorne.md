# Cassian Thorne — Tier B

**Identity:** 27 · male · Dawn reliquary templar · 4★ · former Bell-Tower Warden of the Cathedral of Saint Luthos, sworn escort to Sister Aveline and Mirel Quill.
**Desire:** Guard the people the Church's prayers forgot to mention. **Fear:** That his faith was built on a ledger rather than a sanctuary. **Past [NEW]:** Five years ago (one year after Crownfall), Cassian climbed into the Saint Luthos belfry during a storm and cut down the great bronze clapper with his greatsword, believing that silencing the bronze would stop the bell from collecting hours from the lowland villages. When the bell rang the next night with no rope and no clapper (`seven_bells.md` #2, the Ropeless Bell), he realized the Church's teaching was a lie. Stripped of his belfry keys, he chose to guard the flooded lower nave doors instead. **Contradiction:** A devout vows-man who took a crowbar and greatsword to his own cathedral's bell, yet still polishes the removed clapper-ring he wears at his belt.
**Arc:** Holds the flooded nave doors with Aveline (2-1, 2-2) and guards Mirel during the Yseldra confrontation (2-5) → escorts Aveline and Mirel with the cathedral folio to the Ashen Lord's Estate (6-2) and braces the collapsing gallery arch (6-4) → defends the dais while Fadyme's name is rewritten (7-3). Part II: stands with Aveline when she confronts the high clergy over the book of offerings.
**Weapon — Tongue of the Ropeless Bell:** squared-tip reliquary greatsword (`reliquary_greatsword`) forged around the iron suspension bar of the removed Saint Luthos bell clapper, etched with witness names along the fuller. "I took its tongue. It spoke anyway. So now I stand in front of it."
**Voice:** Measured, formal baritone with quiet self-deprecating irony; treats Mahmut as a fellow shield-brother who disobeyed a bad order. **Never says:** "The bell knows best."
**Companion story:** 1 *How the Bell Lost Its Tongue* — the storm five years ago. 2 *Knee-Deep in the Nave* — evacuating pilgrims with Aveline. 3 *The Archivist's Escort* — guarding Mirel's folio on the road to the estate. 4 *Witnesses in Iron* — why he engraves names on a blade instead of a register.

## Backstory (director pass 2026-10-18)

- **Origin:** Second son of a lowland stonemason who helped repair the Cathedral of Saint Luthos; entered the cathedral's templar watch at sixteen.
- **The Ropeless Bell (Year −5):** Appointed warden of the high belfry after Crownfall. Watching village registers lose names after every toll, he unbolted and cut free the great clapper in the dead of night. When the empty bronze shell rang regardless, the cathedral chapter demoted him to the flooded lower doors rather than admit publicly why he had done it.
- **With Aveline and Mirel:** For five years he has kept the lower nave clear of Choir Knights for Sister Aveline's refugees and carried heavy archive chests for Mirel Quill.
- **When Part I begins:** He is standing knee-deep in the flooded nave beside Aveline, breaking Choir Knight wards so the last pilgrims can reach the road.
