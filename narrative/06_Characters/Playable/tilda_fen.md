# Tilda Fen — Tier C

**Identity:** 24 · Tide healer · 4★ (First Oath choice) · marsh village healer turned Undercity field surgeon.
**Desire:** Keep the living living. **Fear:** Giving hope she cannot back ("I will not promise a cure" mirrors Kera). **Belief:** Fear takes longer than poison. **Contradiction:** Sarcastic bedside manner, never leaves a patient.
**Differs from Iona:** Tilda treats the living and the present; Iona guides the lost and the remembered.
**Arc:** Opens Rusk's order (4-2), learns the order asks for blood; holds the eastern anchor (7-4).
**Weapon — Salt-Road Remedies [NEW lore]:** her reed staff carries sea-glass vials from the salt road where she learned. "Salt cleans. It also stings."
**Voice:** Dry, blunt, short jokes. **Never says:** "It will be fine."
**Companion story:** 1 *One Clean Table* — triage. 2 *The Salt Road* — her teacher. 3 *Honest Prognosis* — tells a patient the truth Fadyme would not. 4 *The Clinic Door* — reopening the ward.

## Backstory (director pass 2026-09-28)

- **Origin:** Born in a reed-marsh village on the coast road; her mother cut peat, her father mended nets.
- **The salt road:** At fifteen she followed a travelling physician, **Old Marta**, through a salt-fever outbreak and learned that honesty is a treatment.
- **Undercity:** At twenty-two she came to the river city for medicine and found the Undercity's poor had none. She and Iona built a clinic in a storage room with one clean table.
- **When Part I begins:** Nine patients, one table, and a Red Vial Guild pretending the ward does not exist.

## Visual story

- **Look:** tan skin, freckles, short dark curly hair tied back with a sea-green kerchief; hooded oilcloth cape over rolled sleeves; herb pouches; reed staff hung with sea-glass vials.
- **Palette:** sea green, sand, driftwood grey. Silhouette: sleeves rolled, always mid-task.
