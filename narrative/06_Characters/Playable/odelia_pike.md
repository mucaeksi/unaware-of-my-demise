# Odelia Pike — Tier C

**Identity:** 22 · Tide debuff archer · 4★ · harbor watcher.
**Desire:** Close every unanswered track. **Fear:** Stopping before the end of one. **Secret [NEW]:** Her older brother went into the Undercity and did not come back — "I followed the people who stopped coming back" (4-1). **Contradiction:** Shy in talk, certain in the field.
**Weapon — Breakwater's Eye:** longbow with net-knotted arrows from her harbor. "Watch the water, not the ripple."
**Voice:** Hesitant sentences that end in certain calls. **Never says:** "Let it go."
**Companion story:** 1 *Chalk Arrows* — tracking in the Undercity. 2 *Brother's Knot* — a knot pattern on a Glass Viper nest. 3 *The Hollow Span* — why she knew the bridge was mined. 4 *Answered* — what happened to him.

## Backstory (director pass 2026-09-28)

- **Origin:** Harbor-watcher family in a coast village on the salt road. She and her older brother **Tomas** stole bread from the same bakery for years.
- **Tomas:** Two winters before Part I he went to the river city for work and followed a Red Vial recruiter into the Undercity. He drew harbor-knot chalk arrows so she could follow.
- **Following:** Odelia left the harbor and has tracked his arrows since, through the Undercity and onto the eastern bridge, where he had once passed.
- **When Part I begins:** She finds the party because their tracks are "the loudest".

## Visual story

- **Look:** sun-browned skin, dark brown hair cut short and salt-stiff, a red knitted harbor cap; grey-blue wool jacket with rope toggles, net-knotted arrow fletching, waterproof bow case.
- **Palette:** harbor grey-blue, rope tan, one red cap. Silhouette: crouched, reading the ground.
