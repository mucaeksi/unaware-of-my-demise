# Garrick Cinder — Tier B

**Identity:** 33 · male · Ember siege-breaker DPS · 5★ limited (Oath Banner 6) · former Royal Pyre-Lancer captain and siege engineer, now Oakhaven and eastern hills night-watch commander.
**Desire:** Never arrive a minute late to a burning door again. **Fear:** Discovering that the orders he bled for were written by someone counting lives like coal. **Past [NEW]:** Two years before Crownfall (Year −8), he commanded the outer furnace-gate relief during the throne-wars siege that Lord Cael Veyr scheduled to the minute and lost by one hour (`canon.md` F-017). Garrick remembers every minute of the breach and the two household deaths inside the keep (Liora and Aren), though Cael's name has faded from his memory under World Rule 11. **Contradiction:** Looks like a ruthless, battle-scarred veteran who intimidates strangers at a glance, yet digs firebreaks for village orchards and checks every young guard's armor straps before a fight.
**Arc:** Helps Jori and Rikka hold Oakhaven's perimeter (3-2, 3-5) → marches east to jam the Crown Guard siege winches he once designed at the Bridge of the Forsaken (5-2) → holds the outer crater steps at the Altar (7-2). Part II: key military witness when Cael's true motive (rewinding the Hinge to the Year −8 siege) comes to light.
**Weapon — The Late Relief:** heavy iron-and-brass siege partisan (`pyre_halberd`) with an internal flint-and-oil channel along the shaft that ignites on impact, paired with a broken brass siege-plumb at his belt. "A schedule never held a wall. Men do."
**Voice:** Low, gravelly baritone; siege-engineering metaphors (load, timber, draft, coal); dry veteran warmth toward Jori and Rikka. **Never says:** "We have time to spare."
**Companion story:** 1 *The Sixty-First Minute* — the Year −8 siege whose chancellor he cannot name. 2 *Firebreak* — training Rikka's kiln militia outside Oakhaven. 3 *Our Own Winches* — disabling the bridge engines at 5-2. 4 *No More Schedules* — comparing Cael's diagram (6-4) to his old siege orders.

## Backstory (director pass 2026-10-18)

- **Origin:** Son of a foundry-wright from the eastern iron hills above Oakhaven; enlisted in the Royal Siege Corps at seventeen and rose to captain of a pyre-lancer company during the throne wars.
- **The lost hour (Year −8):** Assigned to hold the outer furnace-gate at the keep where the royal chancellor's household sheltered. The relief order was timed to the minute by the court chronometer; when the signal arrived one hour late, the gate collapsed in fire. Garrick pulled three survivors from the rubble with his bare gauntlets, taking the burn scar along his left jaw and forearm, but could not reach the chancellor's wife and son.
- **After Crownfall:** Refused service under the Council of Dukes when they began seizing village grain passes. Settled along the Oakhaven–eastern hill road, organizing kiln-company militias and teaching village smiths like Jori Flint where siege rams hit a gate hardest.
- **When Part I begins:** He is patrolling the orchard firebreaks outside Oakhaven, tracking Captain Rusk's mercenary supply wagons by the smell of Guild lamp-oil.
