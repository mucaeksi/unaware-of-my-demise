# Rowan Reed — Tier C

**Identity:** 19 · male · Dawn shrine support · 3★ standard · apprentice wayshrine keeper of the Whispering Ash Forest and the Saint Luthos pilgrim road.
**Desire:** Make sure no traveler's name fades down to its last letter before they reach a roof. **Fear:** Running out of tallow or dry matches on a night when the bells ring twice. **Role [NEW]:** Walking between the forest wayshrine (1-2) and the Cathedral of Saint Luthos (2-1), Rowan carries a woodcarver's chisel and a windproof brass brazier to recut the letters on wooden way-markers and shrine boards whenever the Whispering Bell erodes a carved name (`STORY_BIBLE_PART1.md` §2). **Contradiction:** Easily flustered around nobles and formal speech, yet walks into pitch-black ash-wolf woods alone every evening with a chisel and a lantern to recut strangers' names.
**Arc:** Helps Brin brace the forest wayshrine and guides the party toward Saint Luthos (1-2, 2-1) → assists Aveline with the cathedral pilgrim evacuation (2-2) → sets up field infirmary braziers with Thistle and Iona outside the Ashen Estate (6-3) and along the Altar evacuation route (7-4).
**Weapon — Wayshrine Brazier & Chisel:** hooked iron wayshrine staff suspending a caged brass wind-brazier of blessed beeswax flame, paired with a woodcarver's mallet and name-chisel at his belt (`shrine_brazier`). "If the tree forgets a letter, carve it deeper."
**Voice:** Earnest, youthful tenor; practical countryside politeness; talks about dry kindling, carved letters and road milestones. **Never says:** "The road is closed."
**Companion story:** 1 *The Missing Letter* — recutting Bram Alder's name on the forest shrine with Brin. 2 *Dry Tallow* — keeping the nave braziers lit during the Chapter 2 rain. 3 *Bell-Ash and Beeswax* — trading candle tallow for Thistle's herbs. 4 *Deep Enough to Last* — carving the company's road markers at the crater rim after 7-5.

## Backstory (director pass 2026-10-18)

- **Origin:** Orphaned in a lowland timber village during the throne wars and taken in at nine by the old keeper of the Whispering Ash wayshrine (`canon.md` C39, C40).
- **Recutting the names:** When theChurch pulled its clerks back to the cathedral after Crownfall, the old shrine keeper stayed behind and taught Rowan how to read the wayshrine register by touch and recut fading letters in alder and oak bark after each bell pulse.
- **Friend to Brin and Thistle:** He has known Brin Alder since they were children sharing dry bread on patrol nights, and walks up to the Saint Luthos monastery garden twice a month to trade beeswax tallow for Thistle Roe's salves.
- **When Part I begins:** His mentor hands Mahmut and Fadyme the First Oath Invitation at the forest wayshrine (1-2) and sends Rowan ahead with his brazier to light the flooded steps of Saint Luthos (2-1).
