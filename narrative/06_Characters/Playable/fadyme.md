# Fadyme Veyr — Tier A

## 1. Identity
22 · female · Veil support-DPS · 5★ free start · heir of Veyrhold through her father, last daughter of the Veyr bell line through her mother · the current Last Bell (bearer of the Last Bell seal; C16, C17).

## 2. Core psychology
- **Desire:** To be a companion, not a mission; to have days that feel like a journey rather than a vigil.
- **Fear:** Being looked at as "a final hour"; her name vanishing before she does.
- **Belief:** Small moments are real even if the ending is not negotiable.
- **False belief:** Hiding the truth spares Mahmut. (Second false belief, Part I: that paying at the Altar will settle the debt; she does not know the Altar is now Cael's Hinge.)
- **Purpose on the road (C33):** to pay her debt in full, by choice, at the Altar. While she hid far from the bells, her slow payment spilled onto others as unwitnessed dead and blank name boards (4-3: "the bell will take the answer from someone else"). She lets Mahmut believe they are going to break the machine.
- **Contradiction:** Precise and honest in speech, a practised liar about herself.
- **Secrets:** The prognosis and letter; that C.V. is her uncle; that she recognized his hand from her father's last page.

## 3. Arc
- **Part I:** "I am perfectly fine" as humor → deflection → active deception → confession. Underneath: she walks to be offered (3-1 thought) → at 7-3 she wants to be witnessed instead (7-3 thought) → at 7-5 she refuses the Altar ("We leave with all five names"; "Then stop asking the living to pay it"). Ends naming Cael "Uncle." (C33, C35)
- **Part II:** From hiding time to spending it on purpose. Her question is whether she will let others pay for her hours.

## 4. Relationships
Mahmut · Cael (family who used her) · Yseldra (the woman who chose her) · Clem, Kera, Hana (keepers of her lies) · her mother (a portrait she is not sure she remembers).

## 5. Combat and weapon
Veil support: *Borrowed Hour*, *A Smile Kept* (protects the team when she is below half HP — hiding pain as mechanic). The curse never penalizes the player.
**The Hour Between Bells [NEW lore]:** the silver bell of House Veyr, carried on a chain (the bell pin Hana notices at 6-2 is a separate family pin). Its clapper is a silver thorn that never swings; it rings when she casts, and the other bells answer.

**Approved look (art canon, `art/sheets/char-fadyme-uomd.png`):** pale ash-blonde hair, violet eyes, deep plum hood with the broken crown pin at the clasp, black dress with silver-lilac embroidery, white scarf. The 5-4 "white strand" is a stark, bone-white lock in her pale hair.

## 6. Voice
Wry, exact, darker humor under courtesy; shifts to short sentences when frightened. **Never says:** "Save me." · "It hurts." (until Part II) · Mahmut's name in anger.

## 7. Foreshadowing / risks
- The opening letter pays off in the Part II opening.
- Risk: tragic fatigue. Part II must give her joy scenes that are not about the curse.

## Backstory (director pass 2026-09-28)

- **Origin:** Daughter of the king and of a bell-keeper's daughter of House Veyr, never married to him. Her mother was hidden from the Inquisition by the Ashen Lord, who knew what the Crown did with Veyr blood because he was the Crown's Keeper of the Schedule (C38); the king met her at his estate; Fadyme spent her first years in the estate nursery the conservatory glass still remembers (6-5 story).
- **Childhood:** Her mother died when Fadyme was seven. King Audren recognized her in secret and had her raised in a guarded royal hunting lodge outside court. At thirteen he declared her publicly and brought her to court, where the dukes tolerated her as a decree-recognized but illegitimate "spare" heir; the declaration opened the last phase of the throne wars (C23). She prayed at Saint Luthos as a small girl, hiding under the pews during the long rites (ch2_road).
- **Court years:** Clever, polite and very good at being overlooked. She learned to read people's faces the way other children learned letters. Mahmut was her escort from thirteen; she made him laugh exactly once in the first year and considered it her greatest achievement.
- **Crownfall (age 16):** Yseldra chose her as bearer and sealed her as Last Bell at the bell altar beneath the Silent Bell; she now stands in for that silent bell's share of the Ledger (C19). The seal saved her from the Hollow host and began spending her hours. When the order came to take her to the First Eclipse Altar, she escaped with Mahmut, lost him, and survived alone in hill villages for four years, far from every bell, keeping the curse's pace with her mother's prayer book as a diary.
- **The last two years:** Mahmut found her. She had already planned to leave for the altar, to pay what the villages were paying for her. A physician's prognosis, written in the prayer book, says one month. She decides to make it a journey, and writes the letter (C33, C34).
- **Secret shape of her mind:** She knows her uncle's handwriting from her father's last page, which is Cael's unanswered letter; she took it from the king's study on Crownfall night (C24). She has never said the name Cael aloud, and without the page even she would struggle to hold it (rule 11).

## Visual story

- **Why she looks like this:** The pale ash hair is her mother's; in the portrait at 6-2 it is the same color, which is why Hana recognizes the woman before the bell pin. The deep plum hood is a Saint Luthos pilgrim mantle she took when she fled, dyed darker; she keeps it up because hoods make people stop looking at faces.
- **The bell pin (A-004, C40):** her mother's House Veyr pin, a tiny silver bell-shaped hairpin at the side of her face. The hood hides it, which is why the marshal's soldiers hunt "a woman with a bell pin" and she answers "Then she had better keep her hood up" (ch5_road). Hana matches it to the 6-2 portrait; one lies on the nursery pillow.
- **The crown pin at the clasp** (front edge of the hood, above the brow): a fragment of her father's circlet, broken on the night of Crownfall. She wears it where only someone standing very close would see it.
- **White scarf:** worn high so blood can be hidden in its folds (3-3). The silver-lilac embroidery on her dress is fading; the art bible uses it as a quiet progress bar of the curse.
- **The bell:** the silver bell of House Veyr, clapper a silver thorn.

## The letter (C34, SECRET until the Part II opening)

Written at the start of the road, while she meant to die at the Altar; by the end of Part I it is out of date. Folded in her mother's prayer book with her father's last page.

1. The truth: she went to the Altar to pay, not to be cured, and knew it from the start.
2. She releases Mahmut from his oath: "You were never my shield. You were my companion."
3. One request: "Let no one pay for my hours. Not even you." This sets the Part II dilemma: accepting Cael's offer breaks her last wish.
4. Folded inside: the king's last page, Cael's letter in his own hand describing the Rewrite (C24). Mahmut's first Part II lead.
