# The Red Vial Guild

- **Ideology:** Everything has a formula, including life.
- **Structure:** Two wings under one charter.
  - **Archive wing:** Physicians and alchemists who keep antidote records. Kera Voss, the Undercity healers' allies.
  - **Ledger wing:** Led by the **Red Vial Matron**; buys, sells and brews from people. Hired Captain Rusk's mercenaries; taps the Glass Bell through glass wards.
- **Resources:** Laboratories, mercenary contracts, the Undercity.
- **Objective (ledger wing):** Complete a formula whose "last ingredient" is the heir's blood, sold to whoever holds Inquisition seals.
- **Public perception:** Useful and expensive; red glass is a mark of both medicine and poison.
- **After Part I:** The ledger wing is broken (4-5); Kera holds the archive. Kera's unfinished formula is a scientific alternative to Cael's offer.
- **Enemy units:** Red Vial Alchemists, Scouts, Corrupted Mercenaries, Glass Vipers.
- **Symbol:** A red vial sealed with wax.
