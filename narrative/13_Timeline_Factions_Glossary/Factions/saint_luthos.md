# The Church of Saint Luthos

- **Belief:** Saint Luthos, "the Listener", taught that a bell is a prayer the whole village hears. Names spoken aloud are offered as witnesses to the living.
- **Structure:** Cathedral at the forest edge, wayshrines and shrine keepers along roads, monastery gardens (Thistle's order).
- **Resources:** Prayer ledgers and guest registers, which makes the Church an accidental record keeper of the rite.
- **Public perception:** The people's institution; trusted more than Council or Guild.
- **Hidden truth:** Old clergy records named Fadyme as Last Bell Heir and framed the curse as a protective seal. Some clergy knew and stayed silent. The **book of offerings** records every Offering (bell-line daughters sealed to pay the Ledger) as a thanksgiving, never as a death: seven in the old priest's lifetime, and Fadyme's line "with no date" (C17, C18).
- **Internal conflict:** Faith in the bells versus what the ledgers reveal (Aveline's future arc).
- **Symbol:** A bell with a candle inside it.
- **Part I:** Chapter 2 cathedral, the shrine keeper's First Oath Invitation, Aveline, Thistle.
