# The Council of Dukes

- **Ideology:** Order before legitimacy. A kingdom without a king still needs taxes, roads and records.
- **Origin (C25):** The throne wars were the seven ducal houses fighting over King Audren's succession. After Crownfall none could crown himself, so they bound their seals on one cord: the Council is a truce, not a government anyone chose.
- **Structure / leadership:** Seven ducal seats (names UNKNOWN) ruling from the capital, Carrowgate; decisions sealed jointly.
- **Resources:** Grain, passes, tolls, the capital garrison, the kingdom's official records.
- **Public objective:** Restore stability after Crownfall.
- **Public perception:** Distant, taxing, preferable to chaos.
- **Hidden objective:** Keep the throne vacant. A living heir ends their rule and restarts the war, which is why Fadyme's name was erased, why Mahmut's act was recorded as treason, and why they pay Oren's company to finish the Crownfall delivery order: the heir disappears and no duke's hand is on it (C27).
- **Internal conflict:** Pragmatists who want the Hollows handled versus those who profit from a weak crown.
- **Enemies / allies:** Wary of the Church; uses the Crown Guard remnant; unaware of the Clockless Court (LIKELY).
- **Symbol:** Seven seals on one cord, uncut (contrast: Yseldra's cut seals).
- **Part I presence:** Only as the authority behind records and bounties. **Part II:** political layer; the heir's claim.
