# The First Eclipse Cult

- **Belief:** The unmoving eclipse over the altar is a held breath. When "the hour is paid", the kingdom will be restored to the moment before its fall.
- **Structure:** Chain-bearers and sentinels living around the altar crater; no known leader in Part I.
- **Objective:** Guard the altar, keep the chains taut, feed the Eclipse Bell.
- **Hidden truth:** Their doctrine is Cael's plan in religious form; they serve him without knowing his name (LIKELY that he seeded it).
- **Public perception:** Almost unknown; pilgrims who go to the crater do not come back.
- **Enemy units:** Chain Cultists (link allies and share wounds), Eclipse Sentinels (channel pylons).
- **Symbol:** A black sun held by seven chains.
- **Part I note:** Introduced only as enemies at Ch4 and Ch7; one line of in-world context is recommended in a future pass.
