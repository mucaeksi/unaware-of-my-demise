# House Veyr

- **Role:** The ancient bell-keeping house. It bound the seven bells and is the Ledger's guarantor through its **bell line**, which passes only from mother to daughter (world rule 8, C16). Sons are Veyr by name, not by bell. The kingdom is named after it; the throne was held by another line.
- **Why it is almost extinct:** the Crown spent bell-line daughters as Offerings; during the throne wars seven were sealed to stand in for the Silent Bell and died (C18). The Ashen Lord hid Fadyme's mother for this reason.
- **Members known:** Fadyme's mother (the "bell-keeper's daughter", hidden by the Ashen Lord, deceased); Lord Cael Veyr, her brother (Veyr by birth, not of the bell line); **Fadyme Veyr**, the last living daughter of the bell line and the current Last Bell; the seven bearers before her (bell-line daughters; names UNKNOWN, recorded in the Saint Luthos book of offerings).
- **Erasure:** After Crownfall the Veyr name was removed from court records by Council order.
- **Symbol:** A bell pin: a tiny silver bell-shaped hairpin (worn by Fadyme under her hood and by her mother in the 6-2 portrait; A-004).
- **Status of player knowledge:** Name at 7-3, Cael's kinship at the Part I ending ("Uncle."), house history in Part II.
