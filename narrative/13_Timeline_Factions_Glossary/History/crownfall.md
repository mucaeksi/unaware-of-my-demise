# Crownfall (Year −6)

The night the capital fell. Every Part I backstory meets here.

## What happened (CONFIRMED)

1. The throne wars (the ducal succession war, C22) reached the capital. **King Audren of House Carrow**, Fadyme's father, died inside the last unrecorded hour, the Unwritten Hour, so no one knows who killed him (C26). His last page was Cael's letter proposing the sealing and the Rewrite, signed C.V., which he had refused to answer. The Hollows broke into his study; Fadyme's mother's portrait fell as Yseldra's calling bell rang for her, and Fadyme took the last page (C24, C28).
2. The Silent Bell had been silent for years and the Hollow host was inside the walls. The Inquisition was ordered to seal a bearer from the Veyr bell line at once (issued by Cael, as chancellor, under the Inquisition seal; C24). **Yseldra** chose Fadyme (16), believing the seal would protect her.
3. At the **bell altar beneath the Silent Bell**, Yseldra called Fadyme's name with her calling bell and sealed her as **Last Bell**, standing in for the silent bell's share of the Ledger. **Mahmut**, her escort, saw it. The seal saved her from the Hollow host (the dead cannot take what the Ledger owns) and began spending her hours: "a life held against the dark". (C19)
4. A second order followed, also Cael's: deliver the sealed bearer to the **First Eclipse Altar**. Yseldra carried it from the Inquisition; **Marshal Oren** signed it and ordered Mahmut to escort her. Mahmut defied it and fled the capital with her. Yseldra did not yet know what the Altar would do with a sealed bearer (LIKELY).
5. **Cael Veyr**, the royal chancellor and Fadyme's maternal uncle, stepped out of recorded time with the last unrecorded hour, the **Unwritten Hour**. The court recorded him dead.
6. Mahmut and Fadyme were separated during the flight. The Council of Dukes recorded Mahmut's act as treason; he hammered the royal crest off his armor.

## Aftermath

- Fadyme survived six years because she lived far from every bell (world rule 9); the seven bearers before her were held at the Altar and spent in about ten years together (C18).
- The seven dukes, unable to crown any one of themselves, formed the Council of Dukes as a truce and took the capital (C25). The Inquisition was broken; Yseldra left it as "former" Royal Inquisitor.
- The Veyr name was scraped from court records; Mirel, then a court scribe, copied erasure orders without knowing whose names they were.
- Fadyme's mother had been hidden by the Ashen Lord, Lord Edric Aske, the Crown's Keeper of the Schedule, long before Crownfall; her portrait remained in his gallery without its nameplate. That first winter he refused Cael the Schedule, sealed himself in his archive and died there (C38).
