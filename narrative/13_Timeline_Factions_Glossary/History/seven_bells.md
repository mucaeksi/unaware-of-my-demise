# The Seven Bells

Each bell is a ledger point of the Law of Hours. Six sites lie on or near the Part I route; the seventh is the silenced bell in the capital. Bell names are production names; the player learns them gradually.

| # | Bell | Site | Part I appearance | State | Player knows |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Whispering Bell** | Wayshrine, Whispering Ash Forest | Carved names losing letters; the Ash-Maw's cracked jaw-bell is a shard of it (1-5) | Cracked, still counting | Ch1 (unnamed) |
| 2 | **The Ropeless Bell** | Tower of Saint Luthos | Rings with no rope or clapper (2-1) | Active | Ch2 |
| 3 | **The Harvest Bell** | Oakhaven, recast in the Flint forge (the count passed into their bronze, world rule 7) | "A Smith's Last Bell" (3-2); village names vanish (3-1) | Active | Ch3 |
| 4 | **The Glass Bell** | Lowest cistern of the Undercity beneath Glassford | Glass wards "drinking time" (6-3 reference) | Tapped by the Red Vial ledger wing | Ch4/6 (as wards) |
| 5 | **The Forsaken Bell** | Signal tower, Bridge of the Forsaken | Signal bell disabled by Sable (5-3); bell pulse (5-4) | Damaged | Ch5 |
| 6 | **The Silent Bell** | The capital, Carrowgate; its bell altar is where Fadyme was sealed (C19) | Not seen; a shard is Pippa's charm, which rings at 7-3 | Silenced during the throne wars, at least 15 years before Part I (C18); Fadyme stands in for its share | Part II |
| 7 | **The Eclipse Bell** | Hollow core of the Eclipse Construct, First Eclipse Altar | "The bells behind the altar ring without it" (7-5) | Active after the Construct falls | Ch7 |

## Rules

- The seven bells are one Ledger; a bell is a place, not bronze; shards keep counting (world rules 6–7).
- A bell ringing near the bearer takes hours from her faster (Ch4 acceleration); distance slows payment and never stops it (world rule 9).
- Breaking a bell's instrument (the Construct) does not stop its count: "HOUR STILL OWED".
- The Silent Bell's silence thinned the border and let the Hollows spread. The Crown made bell-line bearers stand in for it; seven were spent before Fadyme (C18). Why and by whom it was silenced is a Part II mystery (UNKNOWN to all characters; design answer to be decided before Part II Chapter 1).

## Not in Part I

The Ashen estate has no bell. It holds the **schedule** for the bells (the ledger page recovered at 6-4).
