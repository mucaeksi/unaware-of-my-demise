# Glossary — World Terms

Approved 2026-09-29 (world foundation W1, decisions C15–C21). Every script, lore file, prompt and design doc uses these meanings. Rules: `world_rules.md`. Facts: `canon.md`. In-game text is English; these definitions are for writers and are not shown to the player as written.

| Term | Meaning | In-world usage | First in player-facing Part I text |
|---|---|---|---|
| **hour** | The substance of a life: counted, given, taken, owed (rule 1). | Everyday speech. | Opening |
| **the Ledger** | The single account of every life's hours, kept by the seven bells (rule 6). | Scholars, clergy, Inquisitors; commoners say "the count". | 6-4 / 7-3 ("the ledger") |
| **witness** | A name that remembers an hour. No witness, no record (rules 3, 6). | Saint Luthos: names spoken aloud are witnesses. | Companion stories (Iona, Mirel, Vespera) |
| **owed hour / debt** | An hour the Ledger expected and did not receive. | "HOUR STILL OWED"; the owing-bell flower. | 7-5 |
| **Hollow** | A dead person whose hours could not be closed because no name witnessed them (rule 6). | Everyone; commonly believed to be punishment for the throne wars (FALSE). | Ch1 |
| **bell** | An entry in the Ledger: a site, a name, a count. Not the bronze (rule 7). | Commoners think the bronze is the bell. | Ch1 |
| **shard** | A broken piece of a bell; still carries its bell's count and trembles when that debt moves (rule 7). | "A bell that stopped" (Pippa's charm). | 1-5 (jaw-bell), 3-4 (charm) |
| **the Seven Bells** | Whispering, Ropeless, Harvest, Glass, Forsaken, Silent, Eclipse (`history/seven_bells.md`). | Production names; the player learns them gradually. | Ch1–7 |
| **the Silent Bell** | The capital's bell, silenced in the throne wars at least 15 years before Part I; who silenced it and why is UNKNOWN (C18). | "The bell that stopped", "the silenced bell". | Pippa companion 2 |
| **bell altar** | The rite stone at a bell site where a bearer is called and sealed. The one that matters is beneath the Silent Bell in the capital, where Fadyme was sealed at Crownfall (C19). | Crownfall memories: "the altar that night". | Confession (Mahmut) |
| **the Altar** | The Altar of the First Eclipse in the crown-shaped crater: where the Ledger was first held open and where bearers are settled. | "The road ends at the altar." | Opening |
| **the bell line** | Veyr blood that passes only from mother to daughter; the Ledger's guarantor (rule 8, C16). Sons are Veyr by name, not by bell. | Old records: "the bell-keeper's daughters"; Church: "the heirs". | 6-5 ("the bell-keeper's daughter") |
| **the Last Bell** | Title of a bell-line daughter sealed to the Ledger: the seven bells count, the Last Bell pays (C17). | Clergy records: "Last Bell Heir". | Not yet spoken in Part I (sheet tagline, GDD class name) |
| **bearer** | The living Last Bell. Fadyme is the current bearer. | Inquisition term. | 7-5 (Vespera) |
| **the Last Bell seal** | The binding that ties a bearer's hours to the Ledger; what people call Fadyme's "curse". | "The curse", "the Fading". | Opening (as "curse") |
| **heir** | Two in-world meanings: heir to the throne, and the Church/Inquisition's "heir" of the bell line. Fadyme is both. Writers must know which one each line means. | Both, often confused in-world. | Throughout |
| **the Offering** | Sealing a bell-line daughter so her hours pay a debt the kingdom cannot pay (C17). | Church: an offering, thanked, never mourned. | Opening ("chosen its offering") |
| **book of offerings** | The Saint Luthos register of Offerings; seven entries in the old priest's lifetime; Fadyme's line entered "with no date". | Clergy only. | Aveline companion 2 |
| **the Sevenfold Crown** | The old compact by which the throne took custody of the seven bells from House Veyr and ruled "by the bells": the bells, the Inquisition, the Church rites and the Altar together (C17). | Formal and court language. | Not yet spoken in Part I |
| **the First Eclipse** | Generations ago, the first Offering at the Altar. The sun stopped at that moment and still hangs over the crater: an account held open. | "The black sun"; the cult's "held breath". | 4-4 (cult), Ch7 |
| **calling bell** | An Inquisitor's handbell used to call and seal a bearer; stays tuned to her (rule 10, C21). Yseldra's is cracked, its clapper tied with red thread, and it still sounds. | Inquisition. | Opening (the distant bell) |
| **the Unwritten Hour** | Only this: the last unrecorded hour before Crownfall, which Cael removed from the Ledger and keeps (rule 4). The king died inside it, so no one knows who killed him (rule 11, C26). | Unknown to the public. | Implied in the ending ("where my missing hour went") |
| **the Rewrite** | Cael's plan: pay enough hours to rewrite the Unwritten Hour and return Veyrhold to the minute before Crownfall. This is his public promise; his hidden aim goes further (C20, SECRET). | Cael's word. | Part II |
| **the Hinge** | The Eclipse Construct: Cael's mechanism around the Eclipse Bell that uses the stopped moment of the First Eclipse to make the Altar count backward (C20). | "The hinge" in the First Eclipse record. | Construct bestiary text; 7-4 ("counting backward") |
| **Keeper of the Schedule** | The Sevenfold Crown office that timed the bells' collections and every Offering; last held by the Ashen Lord, Lord Edric Aske of House Aske (C38). | Court language; the name is erased. | Not in Part I |
| **the Schedule** | The order in which the bells route the bearer's hours toward the Altar; one page was recovered at the Ashen estate. | — | 5-5, 6-5 |
| **House Carrow** | The reigning dynasty. The last king, **Audren**, had no legitimate children; he recognized Fadyme by decree (C22, C23). | "The old king", "King Audren". | 2-3 ("the old king") |
| **the throne wars** | The ducal succession war over a king with no legitimate heir; lasted well over a decade and ended at Crownfall (C22). | Everyone. | Ch1 onward (as background) |
| **the Council of Dukes** | The truce of the seven ducal houses after Crownfall: seven seals on one cord, the throne kept empty because any living heir restarts the war (C25). | "The Council." | Ch2 onward |
| **Carrowgate** | The capital: seat of House Carrow and now of the Council of Dukes; the Silent Bell and its bell altar (C30). | "The capital." | Not yet named on screen |
| **Glassford** | The river city above the Undercity: glassworks, the Red Vial Guild's seat, the Glass Bell in its lowest cistern (C30). | "The river city." | Not yet named on screen (`ch4_road` "The River City") |
| **the Six Manners** | The in-world meaning of the six elements: the ways the living spend their hours — burn (Ember), carry (Gale), hold (Stone), return (Tide), witness (Dawn), hide (Veil) (C31, `elements.md`). | Church teaching; loose everyday speech ("a Veil life"). | Not yet in Part I text |
| **the Clockless Court** | Cael's court outside recorded time. | A children's story (RUMORED). | Part I ending |
| **Vale** | Surname for unregistered roadside children. | Common, slightly pitied. | Ch1 (Nixie) |
| **seven-notched seal** | Gray wax mark of the Royal Inquisition. | "Seven notches" as a nursery threat. | Opening |

## Do not use

- "Unwritten Hour seal" for Fadyme's seal → **the Last Bell seal**.
- "crown heirs' blood" for what binds the bells → **the bell line** / Veyr bell-line blood.
- "last living Veyr blood" for Fadyme → **last living daughter of the Veyr bell line** (Cael is Veyr by birth).
- "The Unwritten Hour" for Cael's whole plan → **the Rewrite**; for the machine → **the Hinge**.

## Notes

- The economy item **Cinder of the Unwritten Hour** (`unwritten_hour_cinder`, lore: "a cold cinder from where an hour burned without being counted") refers to uncounted hours in general, not to Cael's hour. Kept as is.
- Stage 3-2 is titled **"A Smith's Last Bell"** (the Harvest Bell the Flint forge recast). With "Last Bell" now a title, the stage name reads as a double meaning. Kept; review in the Part I text pass.
