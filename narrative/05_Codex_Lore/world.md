# Veyrhold — World Overview

Approved foundation for all story, quest, item and event writing. Rules of magic live in `world_rules.md`; approved facts in `canon.md`; chronology in `timeline.md`. Status tags: CONFIRMED · LIKELY · RUMORED · UNKNOWN · FALSE.

## 1. The kingdom in one paragraph

Veyrhold is a feudal kingdom that takes its name from **House Veyr**, the ancient house that bound seven bells to count the hours of the living and keep the dead from crossing back. The crown ruled; the Veyr kept the bells. Six years ago, at **Crownfall**, the capital fell, King Audren of House Carrow died, the royal chancellor Cael Veyr was recorded dead (and his name has since slipped from memory), and Fadyme, the last daughter of the Veyr bell line, was sealed as the **Last Bell** beneath the silent capital bell. Since then the **Council of Dukes** governs from the capital, the Hollows walk the roads, and ordinary people measure their days by bells they no longer trust.

## 2. How people live

- **Days are counted in bells.** Villages keep time by the nearest bell site. A bell ringing out of hours is an omen; people touch iron and say a name aloud so the bell "hears the living".
- **Names are protection.** Doors carry name boards, trees carry carved names, travelers register at wayshrines. Losing your name from a register is feared more than losing property. (Oakhaven's blank boards, 3-1.)
- **Vale children.** Children with no registered family are written into road registers as "Vale" ("of the vale road"). Nixie and Yseldra are both Vale. It is a common, slightly pitied surname.
- **Money.** Silver Crowns are the everyday coin. Guilds run alchemy, carting and road services; nobles control grain and passes.
- **Fear of the roads.** Mercenaries, Hollows and toll knights make travel dangerous; refugees are a constant sight after Crownfall.

## 3b. The bells, the Ledger and the Offering (summary)

The seven bells are one **Ledger** of every life's hours; a death witnessed by a name closes, an unwitnessed death leaves a Hollow. When the kingdom cannot pay, the Crown makes an **Offering**: it seals a daughter of the Veyr **bell line** as the **Last Bell**, and her hours pay the debt. The first Offering stopped the sun over the crater: the **First Eclipse**. The throne's custody of all this is the old **Sevenfold Crown** compact. Rules: `world_rules.md` 6–10; terms: `glossary.md`.

## 4. The dead and the unnatural

Full classification of every Part I enemy: `bestiary.md`. The six elements in the world: `elements.md` (the Six Manners).

- **Hollows:** bodies left by unpaid hours (world rule). They move by habit, repeat old tasks (pilgrims walk, clerics copy, knights guard) and are drawn to bells.
- **Bellbound / Inkbound dead:** Hollows wired to a bell or wrapped in erased records; they serve whoever controls the bell or ledger.
- **Wraiths:** the dead of great houses, held by portraits and mirrors instead of names.
- **Ashbound beasts:** animals that ate from bell-ash; Ash Wolves, the Ash-Maw.
