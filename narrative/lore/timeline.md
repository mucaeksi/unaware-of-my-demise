# Timeline — Unaware of My Demise

Years are relative to the start of Part I (Year 0). Status in brackets.

| When | Event | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Ancient | House Veyr binds the seven bells and, through its bell line (mother to daughter), becomes the Ledger's guarantor. Under the Sevenfold Crown compact the throne keeps the bells and the border between the living and the dead. | CONFIRMED (C16, C17) |
| Generations ago | **First Eclipse.** The first Offering: a bell-line daughter's hours pay the kingdom's debt at the Altar, and the sun stops over the crater. Jori's family forges and later repairs the altar chains. | CONFIRMED (C17) |
| Before Year −15 | The throne wars begin: the ducal houses fight over the succession of King Audren of House Carrow, who has no legitimate children. | CONFIRMED (C22) |
| ≤ Year −15 (throne wars) | The Silent Bell in the capital falls silent; Fadyme's mother already speaks of it before her death. Its share of the Ledger goes unwitnessed; Hollows spread. | CONFIRMED (who/why: UNKNOWN) (C18) |
| Before Year −22 | The Ashen Lord (Lord Edric Aske, Keeper of the Schedule) takes Fadyme's mother off the Schedule and hides her at his estate; the king meets her there. | CONFIRMED (C38) |
| Year −15 | Fadyme's mother dies (Fadyme is 7). The king recognizes Fadyme in secret and has her raised in a guarded royal hunting lodge. | CONFIRMED (C23) |
| Year −9 | The king declares Fadyme (13) publicly and brings her to court; the last, bloodiest phase of the wars begins. Mahmut (19) becomes her shield-bearer. | CONFIRMED (C23) |
| Silencing → Crownfall (about ten years) | The Crown seals bell-line daughters to stand in for the Silent Bell. Held at the Altar as living anchors, seven are spent, all during Yseldra's service (the old priest's "seven in my lifetime"). | CONFIRMED (C18) |
| Year −8 | Cael's wife Liora and son Aren die in a siege he scheduled to the minute and lost by one hour. | CONFIRMED |
| Year −6 | **Crownfall.** The capital falls. King Audren dies inside the Unwritten Hour (killer unknown to all, C26); the Hollows break into his study, where the mother's portrait falls (C28). Cael, as chancellor, issues the orders (C24). Yseldra chooses Fadyme (16) and seals her as Last Bell at the bell altar beneath the Silent Bell; the seal shields her from the Hollow host and begins spending her hours. A second order sends the sealed bearer to the First Eclipse Altar: Yseldra carries it, Oren signs it; Mahmut defies it and flees with her. Cael Veyr steps out of recorded time with the Unwritten Hour and is recorded dead. The Council of Dukes records Mahmut's treason. | CONFIRMED (C19) |
| First winter after Crownfall | Lord Aske refuses Cael the Schedule, locks himself in the archive and dies there; his name already erased, he becomes a Wraith. | CONFIRMED (C38) |
| After Crownfall | The seven dukes form the Council of Dukes as a truce and keep the throne empty; they erase the Veyr name and later pay Oren's company to finish the delivery order. | CONFIRMED (C25, C27) |
| Year −6 → 0 | Mahmut and Fadyme separated, then reunited in a village (Year −2). Veyr name erased from records. Fadyme lives far from every bell, which slows her payment. | CONFIRMED (C15) |
| Year −1 (winter) | Fadyme's name cut from the Saint Luthos guest register. | CONFIRMED |
| Year 0, before 1-1 | Physician's prognosis: one month. Yseldra's first encounter. | CONFIRMED |
| Year 0, Ch1–Ch7 | Part I, about three weeks. Ch4 (about day 9): prognosis falls to weeks. 7-3: the name is written back, delaying payment. | CONFIRMED (C29) |
| Year 0, after 7-5 | Confession. Cael reveals himself; Fadyme names him "Uncle." Part I ends. | CONFIRMED |
