# Visual Reference — Approved Art in `narrative/art/`

Source of truth for the look of the game. The images live in `narrative/art/` (sheets in `art/sheets/`, brand art in `art/brand/`; reorganized 2026-09-30, index `art/MANIFEST.md`). Reviewed 2026-09-28. Prompts for new character sheets must match this file.

## 1. Brand and theme

- **Logo** (`art/brand/brand_logo.png`): "UNAWARE" in weathered bone-white gothic serif, "— OF MY DEMISE —" in spaced small caps below. A blood-red cross-sword runs vertically through the **W**, crossed by a red half-ring (the eclipse arc) with thorned tips. Transparent background.
- **App icon** (`art/brand/superseded/brand_app_icon_512.png`, superseded 2026-09-30): Fadyme in a black-red hood, pale ash hair, red eyes, profile left; red eclipse ring top right; logo lower third.
- **Key art** (`art/brand/superseded/key_art_wide|portrait|square.png`, superseded by the r2 key art `art/brand/brand_key_art_portrait_r2_v003.png`): Fadyme center-front, hooded; Mahmut behind her left; Kera (witch hat) and a smaller pale-haired woman far left; a pale-haired figure far right. Black gothic spires, a **blood-red eclipse** overhead, a stone bridge over dark water, crimson petals/ash drifting.
- **Store mockups** (`Uomd Tester1–3`): taglines in use — "Some bells ring for the living. Others… for the forgotten." · "A journey of lost names, forgotten promises, and a bell that never stops." · "A story that remembers even when you forget." · Part I lockup "PART I : UNAWARE, I FADE". One VN mockup shows the opening line "The road does not need to know how many it costs."
- **Palette of the brand:** near-black, bone/parchment white, blood red accent; per-character accent colors layered on top.
- **Rendering style:** painterly dark-gothic anime, desaturated, heavy cloth folds, fine gold/silver line ornament, cracked-paint texture, candlelit interiors, rain and fog exteriors.

## 2. Character sheet template (all sheets follow it)

Parchment background, dark painted panels. Sections: name + epithet + one-line tagline · full-body hero · close portrait · expression set (3–6, labeled) · turnaround front/side/back · idle and action/spell pose · weapon/equipment detail · materials swatches · color palette (5–6 swatches) · chibi/battle sprite study · story-state variations · mood & atmosphere strip · VN bust.
