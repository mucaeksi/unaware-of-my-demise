# Sister Aveline — Tier B

**Identity:** 26 · Dawn healer · 5★ limited (Oath Banner 1) · wandering sister of Saint Luthos.
**Desire:** People first, questions after. **Fear:** That her Church knew and let the heirs die. **Belief:** A seal can protect a life and still take payment. **Contradiction:** Kind, but ruthless about priorities.
**Arc:** Part I: protects pilgrims, reads the prayer under the ledger (7-3). Part II: discovers clergy records that named previous bearers; must choose between Church and truth.
**Weapon — Lantern of the Last Vigil:** reliquary staff with a sun-disc; lit at every door she will not leave until the last person is out. "The candle is not for the saint. It is for whoever is still inside."
**Voice:** Warm, big-sister tone; prayers become crisp and exact. **Never says:** "It is God's will."
**Companion story:** 1 *Every Candle Lit* — the refuge after 2-2. 2 *The Silent Clergy* — an old priest's confession. 3 *Witnesses, Not Sacrifices* — she rewrites a funeral rite. 4 *The Last Vigil* — her vow to Fadyme.

## Backstory (director pass 2026-09-28)

- **Origin:** A miller's daughter from the lowlands below Saint Luthos. Novice at sixteen, field sister at twenty.
- **The count:** At nineteen she helped evacuate a burning almshouse and counted the children once. One was missing; they found her in the ashes the next morning. Aveline has counted twice ever since, "once for the living, once for anyone the bell might have missed."
- **Field sister:** For six years she has walked the roads with a first-aid satchel and a reliquary staff, sleeping in whichever refuge still has a door.
- **When Part I begins:** She is evacuating pilgrims from the flooded nave while the clergy above her pretend the ropeless bell is a miracle.

## Visual story

- **Look:** copper-auburn hair cropped short under a dove-grey veil, warm brown eyes, freckles; white-grey habit with an armored field apron and a satchel of bandages; open collar allowed by the costume guide, fully opaque. Reliquary staff crowned by a sun-disc lantern.
- **Palette:** dawn gold, ivory, dove grey. Silhouette: upright, staff taller than her, lantern light on her face.
