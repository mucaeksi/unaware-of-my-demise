# Vespera Wisp — Tier B

**Identity:** 23 · Veil mage DPS · 5★ limited (Oath Banner 5) · wandering glass mage.
**Desire:** Understand time without being owned by it. **Fear:** Becoming a room that only keeps an absence. **Secret [NEW]:** She traded some of her own hours to study the Glass Bell's wards; she is tired in a way sleep does not fix — "young and tired" (GDD). **Contradiction:** Poetic and detached, yet the first to warn others.
**Mystery role:** The only character who can judge whether Cael's offer is real under the Law of Hours (Part II).
**Weapon — Mirror of the Last Hour:** glass lantern and focus ring from the conservatory. "Look long enough and it shows you what it cost."
**Voice:** Slow, imagistic; ancient cadence when casting. **Never says:** "There is plenty of time."
**Companion story:** 1 *Lower Cistern* — her first Undercity visit. 2 *Price of Study* — the hours she paid. 3 *Glass That Remembers* — the conservatory room. 4 *Candle-Eater* — her verdict on hour-trading.

## Backstory (director pass 2026-09-28)

- **Origin:** Raised by a hermit glassmaker in a tower on the eastern moors, who taught her that glass is "slow water that remembers light".
- **Study:** At twenty she found the Glass Bell's ward in the Undercity's lowest cistern and let it read her to learn its pattern. It took hours from her; how many, she does not know. A single frost-white streak appeared in her hair that night.
- **When Part I begins:** She has followed the ward's pattern to the Ashen estate conservatory.

## Visual story

- **Look:** long midnight-blue hair with one frost-white streak; tired pale-blue eyes; high-collared layered robe with glass beads sewn along the hems; closed gloves; glass lantern and focus ring; pale spirit-flame motifs (not horror).
- **Palette:** midnight blue, glass teal, candle gold. Silhouette: very still, lantern held out.
