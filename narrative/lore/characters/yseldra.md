# Lady Yseldra Vale — Antagonist

See also `docs/VILLAIN_BIBLE_PART1.md` (authoritative for role and motive).

## Backstory (director pass 2026-09-28)

- **Identity:** Lady Yseldra Vale, 34, former Royal Inquisitor. Not playable.
- **Origin:** A Vale child. The Royal Inquisition took nameless children from road registers as novices because no family would ask where they went. She was given a number before she was given a title.
- **The Inquisition:** Trained as a keeper of sealed records and overseer of bell rites. Inquisitors wear bone-white half-masks at rites so bearers never learn who chose them. She rose because she never looked away.
- **Seven seals:** During her service the bells took seven lives. She kept each victim's seal on her chain and cut it through the middle the day that person died.
- **Crownfall:** Ordered to seal a bearer from the Veyr bell line. She chose Fadyme, sixteen, believing the seal would protect her, and sealed her at the bell altar beneath the Silent Bell, calling her name with her own calling bell. She watched it begin to consume her. She then carried the second order, to deliver the sealed bearer to the First Eclipse Altar, to Oren herself, not yet knowing what the Altar would do with her (LIKELY; C19).
- **After:** Left the broken Inquisition. Tied the clapper of her calling bell with red thread so it would never ring for a rite again. It rings anyway: a calling bell stays tuned to the bearer it called and sounds whenever her debt moves (C21), and that is how she finds Fadyme. She hunts the Altar's servants and the heir, meaning to take Fadyme so far from every bell that they cannot reach her. She is half right: distance slows the payment and never stops it.

## Visual story

- **Look (for the new sheet):** tall, straight-backed; opaque mourning veil over a **separate bone-white half-mask** (Inquisitor's mask, cracked across one eye); black-plum split surcoat over fitted mail; leather gloves with seven small bell-clapper rings.
- **The chain:** seven **gray** wax seals, each stamped with the seven-notched Inquisition mark and cut through the center.
- **The handbell:** cracked brass, clapper tied off with red thread.
- **Palette:** black plum, ash ivory, cold brass. Never a skull motif.
