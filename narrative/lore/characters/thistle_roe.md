# Thistle Roe — Tier C

**Identity:** 21 · Gale healer support · 4★ · monastery bell gardener of Saint Luthos.
**Desire:** Grow something after every burial. **Fear:** Breaking things she cannot mend ("I have had practice"). **Knowledge [NEW]:** Bell gardens grow plants in bell-ash; one flower blooms only where an hour is owed. She is the only ordinary person with practical bell lore.
**Weapon — Garden Before Winter:** thorn crook cut from the monastery bell garden. "Plant before the frost, not after."
**Voice:** Soft, slight stammer that disappears when she talks about plants. **Never says:** "Nothing will grow here."
**Companion story:** 1 *Bell Garden* — home. 2 *The Owing Flower* — it blooms beside Fadyme. 3 *Mending* — a broken ward. 4 *Winter Seeds* — a gift for Part II.

## Backstory (director pass 2026-09-28)

- **Origin:** Left as an infant at the Saint Luthos monastery gate with a packet of seeds. Raised by the gardeners.
- **Bell garden:** She tends the plants grown in bell-ash; she discovered that the owing-bell flower blooms only where an hour is owed. The monks prefer not to hear about it.
- **When Part I begins:** She has come to the Undercity with healing herbs and ends up at Tilda's table.

## Visual story

- **Look:** chestnut hair in two low buns under a wide straw hat; thick wool gardening gloves; earth-stained apron over a sage dress; seed tins on a belt; thorn crook.
- **Palette:** sage, straw, earth brown. Silhouette: hat brim down, crook in both hands.
