# Nixie Vale — Tier C

**Identity:** 19 · Gale ranged DPS · 4★ · caravan scout, a "Vale" child from a road register; free story recruit.
**Desire:** To belong somewhere by choice ("I know. That is why I can choose to."). **Fear:** Being claimed or filed by someone else. **False belief:** Maps are safer than people. **Contradiction:** Talks fast, trusts slowly.
**Link [NEW]:** Shares the surname Vale with Yseldra — no kinship, but Yseldra recognizes the register mark and treats her with odd gentleness in Part II.
**Arc:** Joins by choice at 1-4, leaves to warn Oakhaven (2-5), returns with its people (7-1): "I am not staying behind again."
**Weapon — Windneedle Wayfinder [NEW lore]:** shortbow whose grip is wrapped in the map that "lied" at 1-4. "Trust the wind; check the map."
**Voice:** Breathless, genki; drops to flat seriousness in danger. **Never says:** "I belong to…"
**Companion story:** 1 *The Map's Other Lie* — who drew the false road. 2 *Road Register* — finds her own entry. 3 *Vale* — meets other Vale children in Oakhaven's refuge. 4 *Pinned* — marks the company's route as home on her map.

## Backstory (director pass 2026-09-28)

- **Origin:** Found on the Oakhaven road at about one year old and written into the road register as "Girl, about one year. No family. Vale." (eighteen winters before Part I).
- **Raised by the road:** A caravan company, the **Ochre Wheel**, took her on at five as a mascot and at ten as a lookout. She learned maps before letters and trees before stairs. The caravan's teal-and-ochre colors are the only heraldry she has ever worn.
- **The hawk:** At fifteen, lost in the ash forest, she followed a hawk to the road; she keeps one of its feathers on her hood. Her ultimate, *Hawk Over the Pass*, is named for it.
- **Leaving:** At seventeen the Ochre Wheel was hired to carry sealed crates for the Red Vial ledger wing. Nixie opened one. She left the company that night and has scouted alone since, collecting maps that lie.
- **When Part I begins:** She is following the false map that leads travelers away from the forest shrine, because someone drew it on purpose.

## Visual story

- **Why she looks like this:** Teal hooded cloak with ochre lining in the Ochre Wheel colors, torn and patched; feather pin from the hawk; laced tall boots for climbing; shortbow, quiver, a knife and a map case. Light brown hair, amber eyes, a quick open grin.
- **Distinctness note:** She is the roster's open, bright face. Keep her expressions large (the sheet's laughing panels) and her hood usually back or loose, unlike Fadyme's.
