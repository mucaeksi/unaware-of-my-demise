# Pippa Bell — Tier C

**Identity:** 20 · Gale buffer bard · 4★ · wandering singer who carries names to refuges.
**Desire:** No one forgotten ("I will sing louder than the paper"). **Fear:** Her own songs fading from people's memory. **Contradiction:** Stage-bright, privately afraid of silence after applause.
**Hook [NEW]:** The bell charm at her wrist (3-4) is a shard of the Silent Bell, bought from a refugee; it trembles near active bells. Part II clue to the capital.
**Weapon — A Song Kept in Brass:** lute-sling with the charm tied to its neck. "A name sung is a name witnessed."
**Voice:** Sing-song, breaks into rhyme, jokes that stop cold when things turn grave. **Never says:** "Nobody will remember."
**Companion story:** 1 *Borrowed Names* — the refuge. 2 *The Charm* — where it came from. 3 *Out of Tune* — the charm reacts to Fadyme. 4 *Encore* — a song that becomes a witness list.

## Backstory (director pass 2026-09-28)

- **Origin:** Born to travelling players; learned to sing before she could read, and learned to read from playbills.
- **The names:** After Crownfall, villages began losing names from registers. Pippa started writing names into songs because "a song gets stuck in your head for a week."
- **The charm:** She bought a bell charm from a refugee from the capital who said it came off a bell that stopped (a Silent Bell shard). It has shivered ever since.
- **When Part I begins:** She is singing Oakhaven's lost names back at the inn.

## Visual story

- **Look:** rose-pink curls under a crooked feathered cap; patchwork road jacket sewn from old concert posters; small lute-sling; the trembling bell charm at her wrist.
- **Palette:** rose, cream, mustard. Silhouette: mid-strum, one foot on a chair.
