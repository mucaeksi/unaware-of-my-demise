# Brin Alder — Tier C

**Identity:** 23 · Stone tank · 4★ · forest village guard and woodcutter; free story recruit.
**Desire:** Nobody left behind. **Fear:** The moment of choosing who to hold (1-2 "If I move, the roof comes down"). **False belief:** Holding alone is what strength means. **Contradiction:** Quiet, but the first to volunteer.
**Why she differs from Nara and Jori:** Brin holds a *village* — people she knows by name. Nara holds a *road* for strangers; Jori *builds* the door.
**Arc:** Learns from Mahmut's 1-2 line that holding can be shared; leaves at 2-1 to escort refugees and returns at 7-2 having led others.
**Weapon — Alderwall's Promise [NEW lore]:** axe-shield cut from the alder that shaded her village shrine. "A tree does not stand for itself."
**Voice:** Few words, warm under them, practical metaphors (wood, weather). **Never says:** "Someone else will do it."
**Companion story:** 1 *The Road Back* — escorting refugees through the ash forest. 2 *Letters in the Bark* — her father's carved name is losing letters. 3 *Two Roofs* — a choice between two shelters; she finally asks for help. 4 *Keep Up* — reunion with the company before 7-2.

## Backstory (director pass 2026-09-28)

- **Origin:** Born in an alder-wood village on the Whispering Ash forest road. Her father **Bram Alder** was the village woodcutter and its informal guard; her mother kept the wayshrine register.
- **The roof:** When Brin was seventeen, a Hollow pack came through during the first winter after Crownfall. Bram braced the burning roof of the woodcutters' shelter so the children could get out. He held it too long. This is why, at 1-2, Brin says "If I move, the roof comes down."
- **After:** She took her father's post at eighteen, cut her axe-shield from the alder that shaded the shrine, and began braiding her hair the way her mother did before long patrols. She talks little because Bram talked for both of them, and she has not found a way to fill the silence.
- **When Part I begins:** She has held the forest road alone for five years. Mahmut's "You do not have to hold it alone" is the first time anyone has said it to her.

## Visual story

- **Why she looks like this:** The pine-green cloak is the village guard's; the fur collar is from a wolf that took two of the village's goats, and she is quietly proud of it. Quilted gambeson because plate is for people with smiths. Long brown braid, practical boots with guards for rough ground.
- **Signature objects:** hand axe and round iron buckler, alder wood under the leather.
- **Sheet notes that match story:** "Rescue Pose" (bracing a beam over children) is the 1-2 image and her father's death rewritten as a survival.
