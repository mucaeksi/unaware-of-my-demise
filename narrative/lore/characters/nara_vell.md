# Nara Vell — Tier C

**Identity:** 27 · Stone tank support · 5★ limited (Oath Banner 3) · bridge gatekeeper; family crests on her knee guard.
**Desire:** Everyone crosses. **Fear:** Being the stone that is not enough. **Family [NEW]:** The Vell family were hereditary bridge wardens; Oren trained under her mother. She saw him stay on the span. **Contradiction:** Calm and commanding, never gives an order she would not take.
**Differs from Brin/Jori:** Nara guards a crossing for strangers and thinks in seconds and distances.
**Weapon — Gatewarden's Promise:** glaive and compact kite shield of the Vell wardens. "First thing the stone hits."
**Voice:** Measured, numbers and timings, protective before polite. **Never says:** "Run."
**Companion story:** 1 *The Measure* — Vell warden tradition. 2 *Oren's Teacher* — her mother's lesson Oren forgot. 3 *Last Cart* — a crossing she could not save. 4 *The Narrow Gate* — whether she tells Mahmut where Oren went.

## Backstory (director pass 2026-09-28)

- **Origin:** The Vell family have been hereditary wardens of the Bridge of the Forsaken for five generations. Her mother trained Oren there.
- **The last cart:** Three winters before Part I, a span dropped with a refugee cart still on it. Nara counted the wheels and could not reach it. She has measured every crossing since.
- **When Part I begins:** She is holding the bridge gate for civilians while Oren's soldiers hold the span.
