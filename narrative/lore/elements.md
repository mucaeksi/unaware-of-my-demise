# The Six Manners — Elements in the World

Approved 2026-09-29 (world foundation W4, decision C31). The six combat elements are the in-world **Six Manners**: an old Saint Luthos teaching that the living spend their hours in six ways. Gameplay rules (multipliers, statuses) stay in `content/battle_rules_part1.json`; this file gives them meaning. No data changes.

| Element | Manner | What it does to an hour | Status (gameplay) | Feels like | Roster |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ember** | to burn | Spends hours fast and bright | Burn | Forge, kiln, alchemy, recklessness | Kera, Rikka, Jori |
| **Gale** | to carry | Moves hours along: roads, songs, seeds, rhythm | Gust (delay) | Travel, messages, seasons | Nixie, Pippa, Thistle, Marek |
| **Stone** | to hold | Keeps hours in place; stands between | Fracture | Walls, gates, oaths, bracing | Mahmut, Brin, Nara, Hana |
| **Tide** | to return | Brings hours back: healing, tracking, homecoming | Soaked | Rivers, harbors, the lost found | Tilda, Odelia, Iona |
| **Dawn** | to witness | Names and records hours so they count | Sanctuary | Candles, prayers, written names | Aveline, Mirel |
| **Veil** | to hide | Keeps hours out of sight, borrowed or unspoken | Dread | Secrets, erasure, the curse, the dead without names | Fadyme, Sable, Clem, Vespera |

## The cycle

- **Ember → Gale:** fire consumes what is carried.
- **Gale → Stone:** wind wears stone down.
- **Stone → Tide:** stone dams the water.
- **Tide → Ember:** water quenches fire.
- **Dawn ↔ Veil:** witnessing and hiding undo each other. This is the game's central theme as a combat rule: names spoken against names erased.

## World use

- The Church teaches the Six Manners as a way to judge a life ("she spent her hours holding" = a guardian). Common speech uses them loosely: "an Ember temper", "a Veil life".
- **Hollows are mostly Veil** in data: unwitnessed dead, hidden from the Ledger. Choir Knights are Dawn (they still sing witness without breath).
- **Fadyme is Veil** because the curse is a hidden debt and she hides it; her arc moves from hiding toward being witnessed. Her skills stay Veil; the tension with Dawn (Aveline, Mirel) is the point.
- **Aether Ward** (7-5) is not a seventh Manner: it is the Hinge running on borrowed, stopped time (boss-only, as in the Story Bible).
- Colors follow `docs/ART_BIBLE.md` (Ember rust/orange, Tide blue-green, Gale desaturated teal, Stone moss/iron, Dawn ivory/gold, Veil plum/silver).
- Player-facing text: not yet in Part I. A codex entry can be added in the content pass.
