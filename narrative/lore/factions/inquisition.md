# The Royal Inquisition (broken)

- **Ideology:** The kingdom survives if the bells are fed correctly. Truth is kept sealed, not shared.
- **Structure:** Inquisitors under the Crown; each held sealed records and oversaw bell rites. Mark: the seven-notched gray wax seal.
- **Objective (old):** Select, seal and deliver bell-line bearers for the Offering; keep the rite secret. An Inquisitor calls and seals a bearer at a bell altar with a **calling bell**, which stays tuned to her for life (world rule 10, C21).
- **Public perception:** Feared. "Seven notches" is a nursery threat.
- **After Crownfall:** Broken by the Council; most inquisitors dead or scattered. Their seals still circulate on old orders, which is why Rusk's order and the altar chains carry them.
- **Key member:** **Yseldra Vale**, former inquisitor. She chose Fadyme as bearer; seven lives were taken by the bells during her service.
- **Internal conflict:** Believers in the rite versus those, like Yseldra, who came to see it as murder.
- **Symbol:** Seven-notched gray seal; a bell cut through the middle.
