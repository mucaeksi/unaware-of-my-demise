# The Clockless Court (Part II)

- **What it is:** The hidden court of Lord Cael Veyr, outside ordinary time. Courtiers do not age while they remain.
- **Part I knowledge:** Only the name, from Cael's offer. In-world it survives as a children's story.
- **Design rules:** Obeys the Law of Hours. Nothing there can take an hour without consent and a witness.
- **Open (to decide before Part II outline):** Its location (the upward-drifting ash is the trail), who serves there, whether the Silent Bell is inside it.
