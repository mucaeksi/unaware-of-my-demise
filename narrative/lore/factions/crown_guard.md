# The Crown Guard Remnant

- **Ideology:** An order is a promise to the kingdom; obedience is honor.
- **Structure:** Remnant companies under former marshals; Marshal Oren was the most senior on the eastern road.
- **Objective:** Carry out Crown-era orders, including the hunt for the heir, believing the rite would "save the kingdom without consuming its heir".
- **Public perception:** Respected in memory, resented in practice; some companies became toll knights.
- **Internal conflict:** Loyalists, deserters and those like Oren who begin to doubt.
- **Enemies:** Mahmut (the traitor who defied Oren). **Allies:** the Council by payment: it funds Oren's company to finish the unfinished Crownfall delivery order, and Oren takes the pay because an unfinished order is a broken promise (C27).
- **Enemy units:** Corrupted Crown Guard, Crownless Knights, Royal Hollows (dead guardsmen still at post).
- **Symbol:** The crown crest, which Mahmut hammered off his armor.
