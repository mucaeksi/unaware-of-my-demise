## Banner-key-art wardrobe accents

The mildly open outfit group is Aveline, Sable, Mirel and Nara. Their art direction uses adult faces, mature posture and utilitarian medieval construction. The emphasis is silhouette and personality, not skin. Each limited banner should use one character's signature prop and chapter location; the outfit itself is not a stat item.

## Character sheet checklist

Each character sheet should include full-body front/back/side, battle-chibi front/side, face close-up, three VN expressions, weapon scale, palette chips, material callouts, boot/glove detail, one idle pose and one combat pose. Story-age details and costume notes stay in metadata, not printed on the image.
