# Player-facing language policy

Owner instruction, 2026-10-05: the whole game is English from the first entry screen. Voice acting is Japanese.

- UI, status/error messages, buttons, accessibility labels, help and subtitles: **English (`en`)**.
- Narrative spoken voice: **Japanese (`ja`)**. English subtitle text and Japanese speech share a stable line ID; neither is translated or regenerated by this UI change.
- Fixed settings rows: `Text & subtitles — English` and `Voice language — Japanese`. No unimplemented language selector is offered.
- Collaboration and design documentation may be Turkish. Player surfaces may not be Turkish.
- Entry text keys are centralized in [entry/copy.en.js](entry/copy.en.js) for future Godot `tr(KEY)` binding.
- Earlier v01/v02 Turkish previews and the archived verification are historical design evidence. Active prototypes and current previews follow this policy; historical source documents and locked media are preserved.
