## Before anything ships

- **Resolution**: every image is 1376×768 or 1024×1024 (≈1K). Mockups are fine; shipped kit and stage assets must be
  regenerated at 2K (ART_BIBLE §3) — via `tools/gen_image.ts` (`size: "2K"`) or the highest size the Gemini app offers.
- **Battle proportions (B3)**: resolved — the owner keeps cute chibi units; the tall figures in the battle mockups are
  layout reference only (HUD, depth, camera), not the unit style.
- Text, stats and skill names in mockups are layout placeholders; the game renders real data.
