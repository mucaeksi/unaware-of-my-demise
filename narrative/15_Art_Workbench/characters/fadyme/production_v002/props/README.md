# Fadyme props v002

Eight props on a transparent master with independent AtlasTexture resources: chain bell weapon with fixed silver thorn clapper, separate tiny maternal hairpin, broken circlet fragment, prayer book, letter/glove, hidden-blood scarf, handless watch, and a new candidate mother painting with cut-away nameplate. `weapon_fadyme_signature` is an existing ID; other prop labels are descriptive workbench keys, not added stable game IDs. No Part II letter wording is baked into art. The mother face is newly proposed, not canon-approved; same ash-pale hair and the family bell pin come from the existing script.

The PNG is straight RGBA; RGB under zero-alpha pixels can contain brown. Those pixels remain transparent; do not flatten the source or infer opacity from an image viewer that drops alpha. The former sheet is retained as a superseded draft, not a required asset.
