## 2. Visual Differentiation & Outfit Diversity (Alluring Gothic Court-Scholar Pencil-Skirt & Spectacles)
- **Hair, Eyewear & Facial Structure**:
  - **Round antique-brass spectacles** with a delicate brass chain — the roster's signature bespectacled scholar beauty.
  - Sleek jet-black hair swept up into a high, neat **scholar's bun pierced by two crossed ornate brass stylus hairpins**, with soft framing side-locks.
  - Sharp, intelligent golden-hazel eyes behind her glasses, a wry, precise half-smile, and dark ink stains on her fingertips.
- **Outfit Silhouette (Tailored Bottle-Green Velvet Bodice + Curve-Hugging Pencil Skirt + Sheer Black Stockings)**:
  - **Palette**: Rich bottle-green velvet, ink black, antique brass, and luminous Dawn-gold calligraphy.
  - **Upper Body**: Form-fitting bottle-green velvet court-archivist jacket-bodice with an alluring square/sweetheart neckline showing her pale collarbone and cleavage, brass astrolabe buttons, and fitted sleeves with black lace cuffs.
  - **Lower Body (Fitted Pencil Skirt — Zero Front Tabard!)**: High-waisted, curve-hugging ink-black and bottle-green tailored knee-length pencil skirt with brass filigree trim, paired with **sheer black silk stockings** and brass-buckled black court heels, plus a structured leather document folio satchel at her hip.
- **Weapon & Elemental Readability**:
  - Left hand holds the open *Unwritten Ledger* fitted with rotating brass astrolabe rings and floating ribbons of glowing golden Dawn script; right hand raises her gilded brass stylus-dagger like a conductor's baton.
