# Sister Aveline (`sister_aveline`) — Character Design Specification (`design_v001`)

## 1. Canon & Identity Summary
- **Character ID**: `sister_aveline`
- **Name**: Sister Aveline
- **Age / Gender**: 26 / Female
- **Rarity / Element / Role**: 5★ Limited (`oath_01_aveline`) · Dawn · Rear-Row Healer
- **Origin / Faction**: Wandering Field Sister of Saint Luthos
- **Signature Weapon**: *Lantern of the Last Vigil* (`weapon_aveline_last_vigil` / `reliquary_staff`) — Tall gilded reliquary staff crowned by an ornate sun-disc halo and a lit sanctuary lantern (*"The candle is not for the saint. It is for whoever is still inside"*).

## 2. Visual Differentiation (vs. Fadyme, Kera Voss, Brin Alder & Nixie Vale)
- **Facial Structure & Demeanor**:
  - Mature, warm 26-year-old "big sister" beauty with compassionate yet unwavering honey-brown / dawn-gold eyes, soft bridge freckles, and a serene, protective smile that sharpens into crisp command during emergencies.
- **Hair & Headpiece (Strictly Distinct from Fadyme's Dark Hood)**:
  - Short-cropped wavy **copper-auburn hair** framing her face and neck, topped by a delicate, lightweight **ivory-and-dove-grey winged nun's coif/veil pinned back with a golden sun-halo tiara** (never a heavy dark hood like Fadyme).
- **Outfit & Aesthetic (5★ Limited Banner Alluring Dawn Cleric)**:
  - **Palette**: Ivory white, dove grey, and radiant dawn gold.
  - **Upper Body**: Fitted ivory-and-dove-grey sanctuary corset-habit with gold sun-filigree embroidery, an open collar / sweetheart neckline displaying a golden sun-disc pendant at her collarbone, and detached gold-trimmed ivory silk sleeves.
  - **Lower Body & Footwear**: High double-slit ivory vestment skirt with a narrow gold-bordered field apron and a brown leather medical satchel of bandages at her hip, revealing bare upper thighs above white-and-gold garter stockings and ivory-gold sanctuary boots.
- **Weapon & Elemental Readability**:
  - Tall golden reliquary staff (*Lantern of the Last Vigil*) taller than her, topped by a sun-ray halo ring suspending a glowing glass vigil lantern that casts warm Dawn light across her face.
