## 2. Visual Differentiation & Outfit Diversity (No Skirt — Roster's Most Sultry Femme-Fatale)
- **Facial Structure & Hair**:
  - Sleek jet-black asymmetrical bob with one long side-lock framing her jawline and collarbone.
  - Hypnotic, half-lidded pale silver-grey eyes with smoky lashes, a subtle beauty mark below her left eye, and a cool, seductive whisper-thin smirk. Charcoal silk neck-cowl draped loosely around her collar (leaving her face visible).
- **Outfit Silhouette (Zero Skirt / Tabard — Form-Fitting Leather Bodysuit & Thigh Holsters)**:
  - **Palette**: Midnight ink-blue leather, charcoal black, beeswax-amber seals, and dark violet-indigo Veil shadow wisps.
  - **Upper Body**: Ultra-alluring, skin-tight midnight ink-blue leather halter-bodysuit with a bold plunging keyhole cleavage cutout and bare side-waist cutouts, paired with an off-the-shoulder cropped black leather shrug slipping down her bare shoulders and long fingerless assassin gloves.
  - **Lower Body (No Skirt!)**: High-cut V-hip leather bodysuit silhouette with exposed hips and upper thighs, criss-crossed with leather dagger-holster garter straps and a low-slung chain belt strung with broken beeswax-amber courier seals, flowing into skin-tight thigh-high dark leather stealth boots.
- **Weapon & Elemental Readability**:
  - Reverse-grip twin curved daggers (*Quietus at the Broken Bell*) linked by a frayed cut bell-rope trailing dark violet-blue Veil shadow smoke.
