# Rikka Ember (`rikka_ember`) — Character Design Specification (`design_v001`)

## 1. Canon & Identity Summary
- **Character ID**: `rikka_ember`
- **Name**: Rikka Ember
- **Age / Gender**: 18 / Female
- **Rarity / Element / Role**: 4★ · Ember · Front-Row Bruiser DPS (`oath_03_nara`)
- **Origin / Faction**: Eastern Hills Kiln-Town Festival Spear-Guard & Militia
- **Signature Weapon**: *Procession's First Spark* (`weapon_rikka_ember_signature` / `spear`) — Crimson-lacquered festival spear with a glowing ember-forged tip and a red-and-gold braided tassel of ringing brass procession bells (*"Loud enough to be heard by the living"*).

## 2. Visual Differentiation & Outfit Diversity (Kiln-Festival Lamellar Crop-Armor & Asymmetrical Legwear — No Skirt)
- **Hair & Facial Structure**:
  - Bright, fiery **crimson-red hair styled in TWO thick twin braids** bound at the tips with golden festival cords and small brass bells.
  - Fiery ruby-amber eyes, a cute smudge of kiln soot on her cheek, and a wide, fearless, boastful toothy grin.
- **Outfit Silhouette (Eastern Kiln-Festival Lamellar Crop-Armor + Braided Gold Rope Obi + Asymmetrical Leg Armor)**:
  - **Palette**: Lacquered ember-crimson, brick red, cream linen, and bright festival gold (distinct from Kera Voss's dark leather brown/orange).
  - **Upper Body**: Sleeveless crimson-and-gold lacquered lamellar crop-cuirass tied with thick braided festival-gold ropes over a cream wrap-top, exposing her toned shoulders, arms, and athletic midriff. One crimson lamellar shoulder guard on her left shoulder and red-and-gold gauntlets on her forearms.
  - **Lower Body (Zero Skirt!)**: Fitted brick-red festival battle-bloomers/culottes cinched at the waist with a thick golden festival rope-knot (`obi` cord) hung with small brass bells, paired with **asymmetrical legwear** (left leg in a thigh-high dark stocking with a crimson-gold lamellar shin greave; right leg bare thigh and knee with a laced festival ankle-boot).
- **Weapon & Elemental Readability**:
  - Long festival battle-spear (*Procession's First Spark*) trailing bright Ember sparks and flames from its blade, with a red-and-gold bell tassel ringing beneath the spearhead.
