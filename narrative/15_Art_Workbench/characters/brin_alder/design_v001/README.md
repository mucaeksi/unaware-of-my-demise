# Brin Alder (`brin_alder`) — Character Design Specification (`design_v001`)

## 1. Canon & Identity Summary
- **Character ID**: `brin_alder`
- **Name**: Brin Alder
- **Age / Gender**: 23 / Female
- **Rarity / Element / Role**: 4★ · Stone · Front-Row Tank (`free_story_recruit_and_banner`)
- **Origin / Faction**: Whispering Ash Forest Road — Alder-Wood Village Guard & Woodcutter
- **Signature Weapon**: *Alderwall's Promise* (`weapon_brin_alder_signature` / `axe_shield`) — Heavy steel woodcutter's battle hand-axe paired with an iron-rimmed round buckler shield cut from the ancient alder tree that shaded her village shrine (*"A tree does not stand for itself"*).

## 2. Visual Differentiation (vs. Fadyme & Kera Voss)
- **Facial Structure & Expression**:
  - Distinct from Fadyme's weary, pale narrow visage and Kera Voss's sharp, sassy upturned tsundere features.
  - Brin has a warm, noble, slightly sun-kissed complexion with subtle bridge freckles, expressive forest-green/hazel eyes with steady, protective warmth beneath a stoic surface, and composed, resolute brows.
- **Hair & Silhouette**:
  - Thick, rich chestnut-brown hair woven into a long, heavy braid resting over one shoulder (braided the way her mother taught her before long patrols), with natural windswept locks framing her face. No hat or hood, giving her an immediately distinct open-headed silhouette coated at the shoulders by a wolf-fur collar.
- **Outfit & Aesthetic (Heroic & Alluring Forest Shield-Maiden)**:
  - **Upper Body**: Fitted dark-leather and quilted ash-linen gambeson corset-vest with bronze buckles and an open V-neckline / exposed toned shoulders that balance rugged frontier armor with an attractive, athletic curvy shield-maiden silhouette.
  - **Cloak & Collar**: Pine-green village guard half-cloak fastened at the collarbone with a carved alder-leaf clasp and a thick grey wolf-fur shoulder collar.
  - **Lower Body & Footwear**: High-slit leather battle tassets and pine-green pleated guard skirt over bare upper thighs, paired with iron-strapped leather knee boots built for rough forest ground.
- **Props & Combat Readability**:
  - **Right Hand**: Heavy bearded woodcutter's battle hand-axe with carved alder-wood handle.
  - **Left Arm**: Round iron-bound alder-wood shield embossed with a shrine-tree root relief glowing faintly with amber-stone ward energy.
