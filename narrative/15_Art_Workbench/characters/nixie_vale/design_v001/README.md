# Nixie Vale (`nixie_vale`) — Character Design Specification (`design_v001`)

## 1. Canon & Identity Summary
- **Character ID**: `nixie_vale`
- **Name**: Nixie Vale
- **Age / Gender**: 19 / Female
- **Rarity / Element / Role**: 4★ · Gale · Rear-Row Ranged DPS (`free_story_recruit_and_banner`)
- **Origin / Faction**: Oakhaven Road Register ("Vale") / Former *Ochre Wheel* Caravan Scout & Cartographer
- **Signature Weapon**: *Windneedle Wayfinder* (`weapon_nixie_vale_signature` / `shortbow`) — Agile recurve wind-carved shortbow with its grip wrapped in the parchment map that "lied" at Chapter 1-4 (*"Trust the wind; check the map"*).

## 2. Visual Differentiation (vs. Fadyme, Kera Voss & Brin Alder)
- **Facial Structure & Demeanor**:
  - Nixie is the roster's bright, expressive *genki* face (turning deadpan-serious only when danger hits).
  - Large, round, luminous golden-amber eyes, lively arched brows, and an energetic open grin (with a subtle playful canine tooth).
- **Hair & Headpiece**:
  - Tousled, windswept shoulder-length warm caramel/light-brown hair with choppy bangs and a real hawk feather pinned above her left ear (from the hawk she followed out of the ash forest at fifteen). Hood is worn loose and back behind her shoulders (never covering her head like Fadyme).
- **Outfit & Aesthetic (Alluring Agile Wind-Scout)**:
  - **Upper Body**: Cropped teal-and-ochre leather scout halter-corset top that leaves her toned midriff and shoulders free for climbing and rapid bow-drawing, paired with a weathered teal half-cape with ochre-gold inner lining (heraldry of the *Ochre Wheel* caravan) and a bronze hawk-pin clasp.
  - **Arms**: Asymmetric leather archer's bracer and fingerless shooting glove on her bow hands.
  - **Lower Body & Footwear**: Fitted dark-leather scout shorts with an asymmetric ochre-trimmed side tabard, a cylindrical brass-capped leather map case and scout knife strapped across her hip/thigh, bare athletic thighs, and tall laced leather climbing boots.
- **Weapon & Elemental Readability**:
  - Recurve wood-and-horn shortbow (*Windneedle Wayfinder*) with parchment-map grip wrapping and glowing teal-emerald Gale wind currents spiraling around the bowstring and hawk-fletched arrow.
