# Part I Route and Travel Time

Part I spans **about three weeks** (C29, 2026-09-29). Travel days match the day counts spoken in the script (`ch3_road` "three days", `ch5_road` "four days east", `ch7_road` "two quiet days"); the script wins over this table. Days are otherwise not shown to the player.

**The clock:** "one month, perhaps less" before 1-1; "weeks, perhaps less" at 4-3 (about day 9); by 7-5 she is close to nothing. At 7-3 Mirel writes her name back, which delays payment (world rule 3), so Part I ends with "weeks" left (W-005). Every day her name is witnessed after that (Iona reads it nightly, Clem writes it daily) keeps delaying the payment.
