## 2. Decisions

### 2.1 Four singles chained by edits (not one 4-up sheet)
Same verdict as the sibling Mahmut group (`mahmut_portraits.md` 2.1): a 2:3 bust must exist as its own 2K canvas (export 768 x 1152 fixed canvas, may not be upscaled, gate A03), a 2x2 sheet would need 4K ($0.151) and could not enforce the fixed crop (gate A28), and one bad cell costs the whole sheet. Singles cost $0.404 against $0.151 and buy the set-consistency gate. Order: `composed` first, owner approval, then each emotion is **an edit conditioned on the approved composed** (bible 3.1 and 6: "approved composed + one sentence of change").

### 2.2 Model choice
`gemini-3.1-flash-image` ("Nano Banana 2") at 2K for all four: the cheapest tier that meets the bible's 2K bust contract ($0.101). `gemini-3.1-flash-lite-image` is 1K only ($0.0336, below the contract) and nothing in the repo shows it holds identity from three references. `gemini-3-pro-image` is $0.134 at 2K (+33 %): used **only as attempt 3** after two flash failures on identity or instruction following. 1K ($0.067) would save $0.034 per image but the bust is keyed and exported at 768 x 1152 from a source whose real 1K size for 2:3 is UNKNOWN (expected about 848 x 1264), so hair-strand edges would have no headroom; not planned (a post-pilot trial on the three edits is a possible lever).

### 2.4 Order, approval gates and the reference path (same convention as the Mahmut group)
1. Dry-run `fadyme_vn_composed` (works today, verified), reserve, execute (max three attempts), review with the checklist below. **Owner approval of the chosen candidate makes it the Fadyme VN anchor.**
2. Approval gate (Claude-owned, not part of these specs): check the real container of the chosen file (`gen_image.ts` names every non-JPEG output `.png`, even WebP bytes; if it is JPEG name it `.jpg` and edit the three paths), then copy it **byte-exact** to `reference/approved/fadyme_vn_composed.png` and record its SHA-256 in the provenance. Until that file exists, the three edit specs fail a `gen_image` dry run with `ENOENT` (verified: references are read before the reservation, so nothing is reserved), while `validateSpec` passes and a copy of each spec with the reference pointed at any existing image dry-runs clean (verified, "reservation WOULD succeed").
3. Edits can then run in any order. Suggested: `dry_amusement` first (16 of 40 lines, and the hardest to read), then `soft_smile`, then `determined`.
4. After all four are approved: one contact sheet of the four at 980 px height for set review, then export (`EXPORT_PIPELINE.md`; not part of this plan).

### 2.5 Light direction (PROPOSAL, mirrors the Mahmut set)
The Mahmut specs put the warm amber rim on the **left** of his frame (toward the screen centre for the right slot). Fadyme sits in the left slot (x = 500), so the rim goes on the **viewer-right** edge of hood, hair and shoulder (toward the centre): one warm light between the two characters, same direction across the set. To satisfy gate A26 (a dark plum hood must separate from a dark forest backdrop along >= 85 % of its contour) every spec also asks for a thin silver-lilac `C.silver` edge light tracing the whole outline; lilac has `g - max(r,b) < 0`, so it cannot create a green fringe for the keyer. The bible's worked example says "warm amber rim from behind"; the side is this file's refinement.

### 2.6 Facing, crop and runtime geometry
`StoryScene.showActor` scales a portrait to 980 ref px tall, bottom-anchored at y 1098-1120, left slot x = 500; the dialogue panel (y 805-1055) hides the lower ~30 %. Hence the prompt asks: head turned slightly toward the right of the frame (Fadyme faces viewer-right, REF composed bust), eyes about 27 % from the top (allowed 24-30 %), hood apex about 4 % from the top (>= 3 %, gate A29), hood and mantle >= 6 % from the side edges, face and the raised gloved hand inside the top 60 % (limit 62 %), bell body ending near 66 % so it stays above the panel line (~69 %), lowest 30 % plain mantle folds that run off the bottom edge. The bible's "subject >= 6 % from every edge" cannot apply to the cut bottom edge of a bust; sides and top are protected, the bottom is a hard cut with no fade.

### 2.7 Hands
The REF busts show one gloved hand holding the chain with the bell below it. It is kept (the bell is identity prop B6) and the other hand is hidden in the mantle, which halves the finger-anatomy risk. The prompt says "one hand ... holds a fine silver chain" and the do-not tail names fingers.

### 2.8 What was deliberately left unspecified (UNKNOWN, bible 4.2)
Skin tone (the prompt never names one; the REFs show fair skin and the edits copy the approved composed), exact hair length (REF: to the chest, written as such), glove colour and material (sheet brown leather, derived bust black, canon guide "thin gloves": the prompt says "thin, matte, dark glove" and the approved composed freezes it; check G13), hood length, bell size ("small"), exact shape of the circlet fragment (written as "a short arc with a few small points and one snapped end", a PROPOSAL; the sheet's tiara is ignored), embroidery **fade level** (CANON says the embroidery "is fading" as a progress bar of the curse; at the tutorial the level is UNKNOWN, so it is drawn clearly visible and no fade is invented), the pale-fingertip rule before 1-5 (default: normal skin).

## 3. Prompt design (all four)
Skeleton of bible 6: deliverable line, (edits only) IDENTITY AND CROP LOCK, SUBJECT (canon identity block, age 22, hex tokens once), one EXPRESSION clause, COMPOSITION (proportions, not pixels), STYLE (R1: thin soft plum-black line, soft cel skin in 2-3 steps, painterly cloth, matte materials, brush grain; mood words only from the bible's list), LIGHT AND PALETTE (cool slate ambient + lilac outline light + one amber rim, darkest `#0F0D11`, brightest `#F2EDE4`), TECHNICAL (flat pure `#00FF00` in all four corners, hard silhouette, no green anywhere on the figure, hair as thick strand groups), and a closing "Do not include:" tail of nouns: text, letters, logos or watermarks; extra, missing or fused fingers; a white streak in the hair, blood on the scarf or glowing lines on the skin; red, burgundy, crimson or gold clothing; weapons, modern items, glasses, frames, borders or other people (+ one expression-specific noun group). The prompt body never restates reference roles (the tool appends `Reference image N: <role>.`). Banned words are absent (no photorealistic, 3D, glossy, neon, chibi, "epic", "masterpiece", artist or franchise names, "glowing eyes", cross or holy motifs).

## 4. Common acceptance checklist (any "no" fails the attempt; applies to all four specs)
Run the keyed pre-pass first (`buildKeyedImage` logic or equivalent; `buildPortrait` does not key green yet, bible 7.6), then review at 300 % on `#0A0610` and on `#D8D2C8`. "bible A2" = `ART_BIBLE.md` section 5 item A2 (letters A-F); "A04" (two digits) = automated gate A04 of `EXPORT_PIPELINE.md` 4.1.

- **G1** One image, one character, aspect within 1 % of 2:3 (A04); real dimensions recorded (UNKNOWN until the first call; expected 1696 x 2528; export needs >= 768 x 1152 after the crop, A03).
- **G2** Flat pure green: border ring median within +-12 per channel of `#00FF00`, ring sigma <= 8 (A07), identical corners; no gradient, vignette, floor, cast shadow, glow, mist or particles; hard silhouette without feathering.
- **G3** Keyer-safe: no pixel on the figure, 3 px or more inside its silhouette, with `g - max(r,b) > 40`; no green rim, reflection or tint on hair, scarf (stays white) or silver; no green fringe at 300 % on either backdrop; thick hair strand groups survive the alpha ramp (A08, A11).
- **G4** Zero text, letters, digits, logos, watermark, UI or badge (bible A2).
- **G5** Identity, bible B1-B8: hair pale ash-blonde (not silver, white, yellow or pink) with **no bone-white lock**; violet eyes; deep plum hood up; black high-collar dress; silver-lilac (not gold) embroidery; high **clean** white scarf; adult face recognisably the sheet's large figure and close-up (eye shape, nose, jaw, mouth); no weapon, no black-gold hand lines, normal skin colour.
- **G6** Three distinct props (B6): one small broken circlet fragment at the front edge of the hood above the brow (not a cross, not a full tiara, not a smooth bar); one tiny bell-shaped hairpin by the face, partly hidden; one silver chain bell with a silver **thorn** clapper hanging motionless. No cross-shaped pin, belt pendant or tassel copied from the derived bust.
- **G7** Costume colours (bible A7, B4): plum hood, black dress, silver-lilac vine embroidery, white scarf; nothing crimson, burgundy or gold; one costume only; nothing from the legacy atlas.
- **G8** Adult-coded and mature (age 22, not child-like), no sexualised framing, no gore (bible A1, A6).
- **G9** Palette (bible A3; gates A23, A24): dark-dominant (>= 60 % of pixels at or below `mist_slate`), darkest >= `#0F0D11`, brightest <= `#F2EDE4`, no pure black or white, no neon, no red wash, crimson share <= 3 %; exactly one warm rim (viewer-right) plus the cool lilac outline light.
- **G10** Style R1 (bible A4): painterly gothic anime, thin plum-black line, soft cel skin, visible brush grain, matte cloth, dull metal; never photoreal, 3D, plastic, glossy, airbrushed or clip-art.
- **G11** VN framing (bible 3.1, A29): three-quarter view, head toward viewer-right, eyes to camera, **eyes at 24-30 % from the top**, hood apex >= 3 % below the top edge, hood and mantle >= 6 % from the side edges, face and hand inside the top 62 %, bell body visible above about 69 % (soft), lowest 30 % plain cloak; the bottom edge is a hard cut.
- **G12** Hands and anatomy: the visible gloved hand has exactly five fingers and a natural wrist; the chain is continuous from the hand to the bell; the bell hangs from the chain (not floating, not fused to the hand); the other hand hidden.
- **G13** Glove colour and material are whatever the approved composed froze (UNKNOWN in canon): for `composed` any dark matte thin glove passes, brown or black, and the owner's approval freezes it; for edits it must equal the approved composed.
- **G14** Curse signs absent (tutorial state): no white lock, no blood on the scarf, no black-gold lines, no pale-fingertip effect.
- **G15** Readable contrast (A26, human proxy): composited on the dimmed forest plate with the StoryScene grade, the plum hood and mantle separate from the backdrop along the whole outline and the face stays readable.
- **G16** Provenance JSON saved (model, prompt, reference hashes, cost), attempts <= 3 for the id, no exact or near duplicate of a sibling attempt (A05, A06).

**Set consistency (edits only, vs the approved composed; A28 plus the bible's "identical crop, lighting and costume")**
- **S1** Silhouette IoU >= 0.95; alpha bounding-box top and bottom within 1.5 % of the canvas; eye line within 1.5 % of composed's; face centre within 2 % of the width; change confined to the face band (mean luma difference inside >= 3, outside <= 4). Thresholds are starting values, to be fixed by the pilot.
- **S2** Same hood and hair silhouette, same pins, same glove, same chain and bell, same embroidery layout, same lighting direction, same palette; only brows, eyes, mouth and a head tilt of <= 5 degrees changed (hand and bell may shift <= 3 % but not change design).
- **S3** The change is visible at a glance when flipping between composed and the new image at 980 px height, and not melodramatic.
