## 2. Spec 1: `bg_forest_whispering_ash_ordered`

### 2.1 Purpose
The single opaque 16:9 background for location `forest_whispering_ash`, ordered state, dusk. Manifest key `backgrounds.forest_whispering_ash` = `art/bg_forest_whispering_ash.webp` (`EXPORT_PIPELINE.md` C: 1920 x 1080 opaque, WebP q80, cap 320 KB, 7.9 MB decoded). Tutorial use: T-1 "The last light lay between the trees" (CANON `tutorial_t1_before_001`), T-2 the narrow path and the pilgrim, T-3 the roof before dawn; all three carry `location_id: forest_whispering_ash`, so one dusk plate serves all (the engine grade deepens it; whether T-3, which mentions stars and "the last light left the road", wants a darker tone is UNKNOWN and a runtime tint can cover it).

Id note: the bible names ids `bg_<location_id>_<variant>`; this id keeps the name already used by `EXPORT_PIPELINE.md` (table C), the legacy reference file and the legacy asset plan (`bg_forest_whispering_ash_ordered`). "Ordered" is the world state (CANON `location_briefs.md`: road clear, shrine intact, light mist), "dusk" is the time of day (PROPOSAL of bible 3.3, supported by CANON T-1/T-2). Chaotic and uneasy states are out of scope (crimson omen marks only there).

### 2.2 Why there is no separate title / menu backdrop
`TitleScene.create` builds the title from three layers that already exist: `art.background('forest_whispering_ash')` (this plate), `art.ui('key_art')` (the locked key art, scaled to full height and right-aligned, about 603 px wide, never regenerated) and `art.ui('logo')` (the locked wordmark at (500, 258..270)), plus a left shade gradient (#0A0610, alpha 0.92 to 0 over 70 % of the width). The key art is therefore reused as designed and the plate only has to sit calmly behind it. The plate's safe zones also fit the title: the logo and tagline sit in the dark top band (low detail), the buttons (x 500, y 640..920) sit on the floor and bottom band under the left shade, and the key art covers the right 603 px (the shrine edge). A second generated image would cost $0.101+ for no new information. **Revisit only if the owner asks for a title composition of its own** (for example an eclipse sky); the one low risk to check at review is the camp-fire glow near the left edge behind the first button (check 14b below).

### 2.3 Model, size, count and why
`gemini-3.1-flash-image` ("Nano Banana 2") at 2K, `16:9`, `count: 1`: **$0.101**.
- 2K because the bible fixes plates at 2K (3.3) and the export is 1920 x 1080: a 1K 16:9 image (expected about 1376 x 768, UNKNOWN) would be upscaled about 1.4x and then zoomed again by the engine (story 1.08, title 1.05). The $0.034 saving over 1K is not worth a soft background shown on nearly every screen. 4K ($0.151) buys nothing the export keeps.

That is 1.7917:1, so the export crops under 1 % of the width in total; every item the prompt places at the edges is held at least 4 % inside, which also survives the story zoom (3.7 % per side at 1.08) and the title scale.

### 2.6 Acceptance checklist (reviewer; any "no" fails the attempt)
