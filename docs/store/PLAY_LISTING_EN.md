## Full description (under 4,000 characters)

A broken oath. A bell in the distance. A company brought together on an uncertain road.

Unaware of My Demise is a gothic anime role-playing game with turn-based tactical battles and a story shaped by memory, names and companionship.

Build your formation, read enemy intentions and choose when to attack, guard or support your allies. Detailed character portraits accompany story scenes, while expressive chibi units bring the battles to life.

Begin with the opening and three tutorial sections, with cinematic scenes between them. Story access does not depend on winning a character through wishes.

English text and subtitles accompany Japanese character voices. The game is designed for landscape play on Android.

Sign in with Google to connect your progress to your account. An internet connection is required for sign-in, purchases, rewarded ads, content downloads and verification of progression and rewards. Cached content may be available when offline; unverified actions do not grant account rewards.

The game contains optional in-app purchases, recurring subscriptions and rewarded advertisements. Wishes offer randomized characters or weapons; view the current banner's rates, guarantees and cost before spending. Paid subscriptions renew according to the terms shown by Google Play unless cancelled. Purchases and ad rewards are verified by the server.
