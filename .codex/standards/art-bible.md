# UOMD art bible and visual authority

This is a consolidated operating standard, not approval of new artwork or story canon. Current visual source/status: `docs/ui/production-v001/source/UOMD_UI_Visual_Constitution_v001.md`. Character identity, locked hashes and change control: `docs/UOMD_REFERENCE_LOCK.md` and `narrative/references/REFERENCE_SET.lock.json`. Current project identity: `docs/UOMD_PROJECT_CONTEXT.md`.

UOMD is a dark fantasy, atmospheric portrait mobile RPG. Chibi 2D gameplay art and detailed anime/light-novel narrative art are complementary modes. AFK Arena is the owner's gameplay/presentation reference; it does not authorize copying protected art or replacing UOMD's identity.

Keep the UI subordinate to faces and artwork: near-black/charcoal values, bone/ivory text, controlled oxblood, selective warm light, matte cloth/stone and aged metal. Use ornament as hierarchy, with quiet text areas and restrained saturated magic. Preserve source document approval status; proposed palette/timing/font details do not become owner-approved merely by being consolidated here.

When authoring or extending an art bible, cover nine areas: visual identity statement; mood/lighting by state; shape and silhouette language; color roles with non-color alternatives; character proportions/expression/detail distance; environments/materials/prop density; UI/HUD typography/icons/motion; asset/export/performance standards; and specific reference takeaways/avoidances. Reuse completed sections and approved references rather than restarting the document.

Fadyme's locked reference set anchors later visual/audio consistency. Never alter a locked original in place; derivatives are new files with provenance. Read the current Fadyme pilot doc for later candidate identities and distinguish pending review from locked art. Never invent powers, costume symbolism, locations, chronology, disease progression or spoiler reveals.

Art-vs-technical conflicts must record both constraints and a proposed tradeoff. Missing inputs produce NOT ASSESSED; no fabricated sign-off. Use `.codex/references/templates/art-bible.md` for an authoring skeleton and the UI/chibi/VFX standards for technical handoff.
